### Gown maker A7a: bead layers on the gown (prototype v202)

- Beads are sewn on the 3D gown, layer by layer: crystals, pearls, seed beads or sequins; as a cascade, on the bodice, along the neckline edge (one neat row, two when heavy), at the waist band, along the hem or all over; light, medium or heavy; 2–6 mm; a named colour or the bride's own, mixed with up to two more colours.
- Each bead is pinned to the cloth it sits on, so it rides the gown as she moves. A fixed seed per layer, so the layout never reshuffles; each bead's colour and turn come from where it sits.
- A phone draws at most 2,500 beads, each colour its share; the designer's count is every bead ("Layer 1: about 1720 crystals, 3 mm, clear."), and `gownBeadCounts` gives the pieces to buy by kind.
- Crystals on a phone use a lighter shader with the same sparkle. No new lights or shadows.
- The eraser's wiped areas (`holes`) are already honoured; placing beads by hand and the lace designer come with A7b.
- Checked against prototype v202: every set, its colour, count, size and where each bead sits, and the designer's line word for word.
