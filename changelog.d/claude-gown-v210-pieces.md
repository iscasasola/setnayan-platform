### Attire Maker: colour and fabric per piece reach the designer's price list, cutting list, quote and order (prototype v210)

- **Price list.** A new "Colour per piece" group after Fabrics. It lists dip-dye / colour fade (per piece), a border band (per piece), and patterned fabrics by the metre: brocade, jacquard, floral print, dots, stripes, plaid, and "from your library". All are unpriced until the designer prices them.
- **Cutting list.** A bodice or skirt in its own fabric is cut from its own bolt and named; other pieces in their own fabric are named once, as in v210.
- **Quote.**
  - A piece cut from another bolt is its own fabric line: "Crepe (the bodice, gold)". A patterned piece is priced on its pattern line, and a shop's own fabric at its own price.
  - The gown's outer metres are only the remainder (the maker takes each piece's share out), so nothing is counted twice.
  - Sleeves in their own piece replace the accent-sleeves line, and a cape in its own piece replaces the cape-cloth line.
  - Each dip-dyed piece is one dyeing line. Each border band is a cutting-and-applying line plus its own fabric, measured along the edge on her body.
- **Order.** One sentence per piece she changed that is still on the gown, for example: "Skirt in red brocade satin, fading to gold towards the hem (dip-dye, from 20 % to 100 % of its length), with a border band 8 cm wide on the hem in ivory crepe".
- **Built on Builder A's contract (#8525).** Its `pieces`, `borders`, `dyeFade` and `parts`; the hem edges now come from its one list.
