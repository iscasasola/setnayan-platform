Note for the owner — not part of the page

What changed from the first draft

  • The page now sits inside Setnayan. The top bar (mark, search, Sign in) and the left rail (Discover · My Home · Memories · People · SetnaProd) stay put, and Ice's page renders in the content area, the way a YouTube channel renders inside YouTube. On a phone the rail folds down into a bottom bar. The first draft's standalone "Made with Setnayan" bar is gone.
  • The identity block is YouTube's shape: banner, round avatar, name, then @icecasa · 3 celebrations · 0 followers on one line, the tagline, the page link, and one clear action: Follow, with Share beside it. In the first draft the name was a poster; here it is a header, and the page reads as one person's channel rather than a landing page.
  • The banner slot (`uprof-cover`) is used. Nobody has uploaded a banner, so the house draws one: warm paper, a linen grain, and the person's own celebration colours (wine, gold, paper) falling across the right as ribbons. When someone does upload a banner it goes in the same slot.
  • Tabs, with no script. Celebrations · Chapters · About switch with plain HTML radio inputs and CSS. Chapters is honestly empty (Ice has published none). About holds what YouTube puts in About: the description, the link, the counts (including the single view), Credits, and "Days you have shared", which for a stranger is a sign-in prompt rather than an invented list.
  • Per-card labels, as you ruled: Maria & Jose carries Up next, Indalecio & Claire carries Coming soon, Movie Night carries nothing. I put the label above the name as a small gold eyebrow, never on the cover, so the gold cover still carries no text. I also ordered the grid Up next → Coming soon → finished; the first draft had Indalecio & Claire first.
  • The cards themselves are the first draft's cards. Same covers, same medallions, same paper treatment for Movie Night. The one change is the eyebrow row.

What I took from YouTube, and what I left

  • Took: the shell-stays-put rule; the banner → avatar → name → handle-and-counts → description → one action order; text tabs with a single underline; a content grid as the body; About as a tab rather than a sidebar; the rail collapsing to icons at medium widths and to a bottom bar on phones.
  • Left: the 16:9 thumbnail grid. Weddings are invitations, not videos, so the covers stay portrait. Left the red, the black, the "Subscribe" verb (ours is Follow), the notification bell, the Shorts/Posts/Playlists sprawl, and the view count in the header. YouTube also shows a "...more" truncated description; ours is one sentence, so it just sits there in full.

What I am not sure about

  • "in 80 days" / "in 86 days" beside the labels is derived from the dates, not invented, and it is the kind of relative time YouTube prints ("3 days ago"). It is one span each; delete `.in` if you want the bare label.
  • The 3-celebrations count in the header includes the finished Movie Night. If "celebrations" should mean upcoming only, it becomes 2. I used 3 because the tab below shows three.
  • Which rail item is "current" when you view someone else's page. I highlighted none. The real page lives at /u/…, which belongs to no rail item; People is the nearest neighbour and could be lit instead.
  • The creator badge and tier chip are designed in (`uprof-badge-row`, under the page link) but render nothing, because Ice holds neither and I was told to invent nothing. The chip style is in the CSS, ready.
  • The year-grouped chronicle (`uprof-yr-*`) is not drawn. All three celebrations are in 2026 and YouTube does not group by year, so a year heading would be furniture. It earns its place once a page spans years; I would add it inside the Celebrations tab then, not as a fourth tab.
  • The rail's own icons, the wordmark, and the search placeholder are plausible stand-ins for the shipped `fd-topbar` / `fd-rail`. When this is built, the real shell components wrap the page and these drawings are discarded; only what is inside `.uprof-inner` is the deliverable.
  • "0 followers" is still on the first line because YouTube would print it and hiding a zero is a product decision, not a design one. It is set in the muted grey and the handle takes the weight.
