## 2026-08-04 · fix(vendor): Papic no longer arrives switched on for photographers

A photographer opening their day-of console found **Papic capture already on**,
sitting among their shot list and the run of show. The owner was plain about
this: *"papic is not used by photographers… it is not a photographer's tool."*
Someone shooting a wedding is working their own camera; a second one, pre-enabled,
on the day, is noise at the worst possible moment.

It is now off for everyone by default, and still one tap away for any vendor who
wants it — a coordinator, a host, a stylist catching moments between setups. A
vendor who has already switched it on keeps it on.

Also corrected a note in the couple's onboarding that said vendor capture was
switched off pending legal review. It has been **switched on in production since
16 July** — the note had been quietly wrong for nineteen days. The couple's
onboarding still talks about guests only, but for the honest reason: what a
booked vendor may capture is the vendor's own decision on their own console, and
promising it during the couple's sign-up would sell something neither they nor
we control.

SPEC IMPACT: None — this is the owner's 2026-08-04 ruling applied; the vendor
capture lane itself is unchanged.
