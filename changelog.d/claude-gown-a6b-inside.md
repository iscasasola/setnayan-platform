### Gown maker: the overlay and what is inside the gown (step A6b)

- The overlay (v195): asymmetric, tiered or cascade, in chiffon, georgette, tulle, organza or the gown's own fabric; sewn at the waist, riding the skirt, stopping above the floor; on a ball gown it sits as tiers. A back cape and a veil hang outside it. Its metres join the outer fabric when cut from the gown; otherwise they are its own line.
- What is inside the gown (v191–v195): each layer under the skirt with its own fullness and colour, the lining, her body shaping, the hoop's rings, the boning and the waist stay, never inside her body. The Inside stage dresses her in order (body shaping, lining, layer by layer, boning) and "See inside" lists each piece with its colour.
- Organza and chiffon layers now get their metres from their own hem and length.
- Dev lab only (`/dev/gown-maker-lab?view=inside`, the Overlay pickers); nothing a person sees yet.
