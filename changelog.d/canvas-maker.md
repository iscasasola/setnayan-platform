## 2026-07-28 · feat(vendor-services): the ZERO-STEP card maker — the card IS the form (flag-dark)

Owner-locked 2026-07-27 (`DECISION_LOG.md`, "🧙 THE MAKER IS ZERO STEPS — THE CARD IS THE FORM"; artifact 15007a4d): *"design the best wizard service card maker. can be lower than 4 steps. can be 2 or 3 or 1. for as long as it feels light and easy to do."* The answer to "how few steps" is **NONE**. The vendor is shown **their own card** — the thing couples will see — and edits it by **touching it**. Each region opens a small bottom sheet; the card updates behind it. Navigation is gone. Design lineage today: 7 steps → 4 → **0**, and every collapse removed *navigation*, never content.

Ships **FLAG-DARK** behind a new `NEXT_PUBLIC_CANVAS_MAKER_ENABLED` (exact-truthy, same idiom as `chat-contact-filter-flag.ts`). OFF ⇒ `/vendor-dashboard/services/new/[category]` renders the shipped 6-step `<ServiceWizard>` unchanged, and does not even run the canvas-only audience lookups.

**THE HARD CONSTRAINT — the server did not change.** `services/actions.ts` is untouched. The canvas is an alternative **rendering** of the wizard's one `<form action={commitVendorService}>`: same action, same 38 field names, same child editors. `commitVendorService` cannot tell which component drew the screen. That is *enforced*, not asserted — `lib/canvas-field-parity.test.ts` walks both component trees (following relative `.tsx` imports, resolving `name={CUSTOMIZATION_FIELD_NAME}` to its wire value, normalising template names to `std-${}`) and fails the build the moment the two input-name sets diverge. The review found a blind spot in exactly that guarantee — deleting the `<DiscountsEditor />` **mount** left all nine tests green, because names collect per FILE via the import graph and `./service-list-editors` stays imported for its siblings (a `\bDiscountsEditor\b` check matched the import line). Now every editor that contributes posted names is asserted by its **JSX open tag** on both sides, with the lexical limit written down beside it: a conditional mount (`cond ? <X/> : null`) is out of static reach, so the one we ship — the flag-dark `<CustomizationStep>` — is pinned to the same flag on both sides and asserted to appear exactly once each.

**Reuse is the whole design; the delta is composition.** Mounted, not forked: `FileUpload` (+ watermark / `compressImage` / `qrGuard` / `variant="square"`) · `ShowcaseMediaFields` · `PricingBasisEditor` + `IncludedFlags` · `PriceBracketsEditor` · `InclusionsEditor` · `DiscountsEditor` · the #3846 merged `CustomizationStep` (behind the same `packageAuthoringEnabled()` flag as the wizard) · `Field` · `SubmitButton` · `useModalA11y` · the coverage chip idiom from `coverage-panel.tsx`. Genuinely new: the card face, the `CanvasSheet` container, and `lib/card-health.ts`.

**Six regions.** Cover (1:1 square, ≤280 px, owner-locked 2026-07-27 — `variant="square"` is the canonical picker) with the 56 px showcase strip + clip pill → Photos sheet · title, edited **inline on the card** · price line → Price sheet · ✦ Setnayan Exclusive → its sheet + the 4 shipped suggestion chips · "What couples get" → the Customization editor + inclusions · audience → Who-it's-for.

**Price speaks the basis** (owner's own words, 2026-07-27; all three read SHIPPED columns): per hour → `₱25,000 · first 4 hrs, +₱6,000/hr` (`hour_base_php` covers `min_hours`, then `extra_hour_php` each) · per head → `₱1,200 per head · min 50 pax` (`min_pax` is a billing **floor** — fewer guests bill at the minimum) · per event → `₱80,000 flat`, regardless of hours and pax (brackets still win the anchor).

**The card moves while you type — the prototype's price sheet did not, and the owner read that as "I cannot save the price"** (2026-07-28). The read is now its own pure module, `lib/canvas-form-snapshot.ts` (`cardPriceLine` · `readCanvasFormSnapshot` · `canvasSnapshotKey`), so the property is testable without a DOM: `lib/canvas-form-snapshot.test.ts` types into a `FormData` and asserts the card's own string, **with no form submission anywhere in the file**. Wiring: `input` + `change` on the form, a **click read deferred one tick** (the basis picker and unit toggles are `type="button"` controls writing React-*controlled* hidden inputs — reading during the click would read the pre-commit value), and a 400 ms poll for the editors that write controlled hidden inputs silently (`FileUpload`, the list editors, the customization draft). **No focus theft:** every field inside a sheet is uncontrolled (`defaultValue`), so a parent re-render reconciles the same DOM node and leaves the caret alone — and the poll now bails on an unchanged `canvasSnapshotKey`, so an idle tick costs one FormData read and zero renders. A new parity assertion pins all of it, including that the card form carries **exactly two** submits (Publish + Save as draft) — a per-region "save" would mean it had stopped being one form. **No price is a HINT (`no_price` → the price sheet), never a blocker** — the shipped wizard publishes "quote on request" too.

**`lib/card-health.ts` — a deterministic quality score, Rule 1 (no LLM, ₱0 per keystroke).** Pure over a plain snapshot. Three lanes and one number: **blockers** (no cover · empty Exclusive · a `findVendorTextViolation` 'card'-profile hit · a pick-N above its option count) · **warnings** (>6 top-level questions — follow-ups don't count) · **hints** (no price · <3 photos and no clip · a choice of one · no event types picked).

**The score now mirrors the publish gate completely.** The review found it text-checked the title, the Exclusive and the customization lines but **not** inclusion labels or discount conditions — both of which `commitVendorService` checks — so "Free album — text 0917 555 1234" typed into an inclusion scored **100 / "✓ Ready — publish"** and was then bounced by the server, while the identical string one field over (a customization option) was caught. `canvas-form-snapshot.ts` now reads `inclusion_label` and `discount_conditions_md` (both in the poll's change key, which also stopped joining on `''` so `['ab','c']` and `['a','bc']` are distinct), and card health checks all five field groups **under the server's own labels, verbatim** — `Title` (was "Listing title"), `Setnayan Exclusive`, `Inclusion N`, `Discount N conditions`, `Customization line N option M` — so the vendor reads one sentence, not two. Discount conditions are read **index-preserving** (blanks kept): the gate reports by row number, and dropping blanks would point at a row that is not the one on screen. **A blocker caps the score at 30 and forces grade `blocked`** — an ad that cannot run has no quality; warnings cap at 60, hints at 80, floor 12. Break that cap and two *named* NEUTRALISATION tests go red (verified: neutralised → exactly those 2 of 26 fail; restored → 26/26). Blank names are never a finding — they are auto-named at save (`autoName`), same owner ruling.

**Exactly ONE progress surface** (owner 2026-07-27, on seeing two bars): the sticky header carries the meter, grade + score/100, an "N items ▾" count that **is** the diagnostics toggle (gone at zero items), and the single next-best-action chip — `▸ Next: add a cover photo`, deep-linking to the region that fixes it, turning green `✓ Ready — publish` when nothing is left. Publish is disabled while a blocker stands. Nothing health-shaped renders anywhere else.

**Sheets keep their inputs mounted.** The shipped `<Sheet>` primitive returns `null` when closed, which is right for a modal and fatal here — an unmounted input posts nothing. `CanvasSheet` keeps the primitive's contract (`role="dialog"`, `aria-modal`, `aria-labelledby`, Esc, focus trap + restore, scroll lock, backdrop dismiss — all via the same `useModalA11y`) and swaps the unmount for the `hidden` attribute, exactly the wizard's `show()` mechanic. Its root deliberately carries **no** Tailwind display utility, because `.flex` out-cascades preflight's `[hidden]{display:none}`.

**Audience = the SHIPPED coverage rows, and ALL of them.** A 13-agent adversarial review (2026-07-28) caught the first cut hardcoding two curated lists — 11 keys against live prod's **16 active** (`event_type_vocab WHERE status='active'`, verified). `celebration`, `gala_night`, `simple_event`, `date` and `hangout` rendered no chip; since only rendered checkboxes post and `updateCoverageServes` is **replace-all**, opening the sheet to change a faith would have saved `['wedding','celebration']` → `['wedding']`, and a coverage of only-unlisted keys would post zero types and get `parseEventTypes`-forced to `['wedding']` — an audience never chosen — with `syncProfileFromCoverages` propagating the loss to Explore. Fixed by `lib/canvas-audience-groups.ts`: the owner's Life-events / Events split is kept and a third **"More events"** catch-all renders everything outside them, so an admin-added type appears with no code change. The **union invariant** (`rendered === whole vocab`, exactly once each) is the headline test; the sheet renders `audienceGroups(...).map(...)` and a test forbids a local `.filter()` coming back. Empty `faiths` still means *all faiths welcomed*. It is written by the shipped `updateCoverageServes`, so it is a **sibling** form — never nested (`scripts/lint-nested-forms.mjs`) — fenced with `parity:ignore`, and the parity test interrogates the fence: it must post to `updateCoverageServes`, must never contain `commitVendorService`, and may only carry `event_types` / `faiths` / `coverage_id`. The coverage *picker* lives in that sheet but its **field** (`coverage_id`) stays on the card form under its shipped name.

**Motion** (artifact 15007a4d, transcribed): sheets rise `280ms ease`, revealed content `250ms`, the meter is a `450ms` width transition — three `sn-canvas-*` classes in `globals.css`, no animation library, all neutralised by the file's global `prefers-reduced-motion` freeze plus explicit per-class guards. Mobile: 16 px inputs scoped to `.sn-canvas` (fixes iOS zoom-on-focus **inside the mounted shipped editors** without forking one of them), ≥34 px tap targets, sticky header, `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)` sheet padding.
