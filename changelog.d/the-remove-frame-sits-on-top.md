## 2026-08-29 · fix(launcher): the "Remove this celebration?" frame sits in front of the whole page, and Enter removes it

Owner, on his phone, with the frame open: *"i cannot click on the delete this frame should be on top and not under."* Then, a minute later: *"also pressing enter on that text box should also confirm."* Both are fixed here.

**The frame was not broken — it was outrankable.** The remove confirmation rendered as `absolute z-50` inside the board card's own wrapper, so the only thing holding it above the shelves below it was a number. A z-index ranks you against your siblings **inside whatever stacking context an ancestor happens to have opened**, and this board is full of ancestors that open one — an animating section, a blurred card, a sticky bar. Clamped inside one of those, the panel loses to the shelf that comes next, which paints straight over its bottom edge: over **Cancel**, and over the one button the frame exists to offer. The owner's second screenshot shows exactly that — the button row half-covered, by white-on-white, which is why it reads as "the button doesn't work" rather than as a layout bug.

**A taller panel is also a trapped one.** Six reason chips, a notes box and the type-the-name field made this frame about 590px. Hung 44px below a card on a ~780px phone it runs out of screen with the buttons at the bottom — and nothing about `absolute` lets a person scroll to them, because the page scrolls and the panel goes with the card. Two different causes, one symptom: *the delete button cannot be pressed*.

**The fix is structural, not a bigger number.** The frame is now a native `<dialog>` opened with `showModal()` and portaled to `document.body`. `showModal()` puts the element in the browser's **top layer**, which is above every stacking context by construction — no z-index anywhere, today or in a future PR, can get in front of it. It also caps its own height and scrolls inside itself, so the buttons are reachable on a short screen. Focus trap, ESC, and the inert background come from the browser.

🔑 **RULE 0 paid: this idiom already ships.** `app/_components/confirm-dialog.tsx` has used `<dialog>` + `showModal()` + `createPortal` since the 18 `window.confirm()` call sites were retired. Nothing was invented — the shape was reproduced. Its own presentational component was **not** reused, because this frame is not a title-and-body confirm: it carries the impact counts, the supplier-agreement arm, the request arm, the reason picker and the typed name.

**Nothing about the frame's words, arms or gates changed.** The confirm block moved wholesale — same copy, same branches, same permanence warning the owner asked for on 2026-08-20, same typed-name requirement. The only content-level delta is the panel's own padding (`px-3 py-2.5` → `p-4`), which was sized for a 280px popover and is now inside a 26rem modal.

**Enter removes it, on exactly the button's terms.** A lone `<input>` outside a `<form>` does nothing at all on Enter, so somebody who has just typed their celebration's name in full pressed the key every text box has taught them to press and the screen ignored them. 🔑 **The second door is never the looser one:** the key handler's guard is the button's guard, spelled identically — if they drifted, Enter would remove a celebration while the control on screen was still greyed out. A guard pins the two spellings as one.

**Also:** the anchored ⋯ menu now stands down while the frame is open (`{open && !confirming ? …}`). Two overlays for one decision is the menu painting under its own dialog. The small popover itself is untouched — it is anchored and correct where it is.

🛡 New guard `app/dashboard/(launcher)/the-remove-frame-is-on-top.test.ts` — 9 assertions, **every one mutation-checked with its occurrence count printed before → after, all nine RED**: unmount the dialog (1→0) · `showModal()` → `show()` (1→0) · drop the portal (1→0) · **move the content outside the dialog while the mount stays (probe 1→1, a position mutation, not a deletion)** · let the menu stay open under it (1→0) · take away the height cap and the scroll (1→0) · delete the Enter handler (1→0) · **make the key handler looser than the button (2→1)** · drop `preventDefault` (1→0).
