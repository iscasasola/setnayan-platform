## 2026-08-05 · fix(partnerships): tell couples what they actually get — and let a partnership change

Vendors can link to each other in four ways. Two of them are real offers to the
couple: **the partner is included in my package at no extra cost**, or **the
partner discounts when booked alongside me**. Both were shown to couples as the
same vague phrase — *"Preferred partner"* — which threw away the only part worth
knowing. A couple now reads **"Included in their package"** or **"Discounted
together"**.

**Nobody pays for any of this**, and nothing here changes that. The words
"sponsored" in the stored values mean the *vendor* is sponsoring their partner's
service for the couple — not buying placement from us. That name has already
caused one reader to conclude the marketplace was being reordered by paid ads.
It never was.

**Two screens disagreed about the same four things.** A vendor's profile page
picked which badge to show alphabetically, so "Accredited" always won; search
ranked "included in package" highest. A vendor who was both got the strong
position in search and the weak label on their profile. There is now one order —
strongest is whatever gives the couple most — and both screens read it.

**A partnership can now change.** Before, the kind was fixed at the moment it was
first proposed and nothing could alter it; two vendors whose relationship grew
into a package had to start a whole second partnership. Now it can be changed —
and because saying "they're included in my package" makes a claim about someone
else's money, that change goes back to them for agreement and the badge comes
down until they say yes. Moving to something that claims less takes effect at
once.
