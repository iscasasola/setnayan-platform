### Gown maker: the accuracy review (step A6e, prototype v197)

- Skirts as they are really cut: an A-line leaves the body at the waist with about 10 cm of ease at the hips; the old shape is now Fit and flare, and a design saved before keeps its look (contract version 2 reads version 1 and opens its A-line as Fit and flare). Empire A-lines, ball-gown hems by petticoat, the mermaid's flare at 55% and the sheath's hem follow v197.
- Trains run straight back and widen; American and French bustles pick the train up.
- Necklines: Straight across is new; the sweetheart's depth runs from straight across to a plunge; a scoop back is a U; the bateau is shallow and wide; a strapless back stops at the bra line; a high neck with an open back keeps a nape band with one button, and the back opens below it as a keyhole.
- Veils are measured from the crown (Elbow and Floor are new); a classic veil's blusher is worn back or over the face; the mantilla has lace all round its edge; the Juliet cap is a lace cap edged with pearls.
- A lace-up needs about 15 cm of back above the waist, and shows an open V over a modesty panel with a ribbon tied in a bow. On a strapless bodice long sleeves are detachable; a hem ruffle is the skirt's last tier, about 13 cm up; fringe and feathers hang from above the hem; a small bow in the gown fabric can sit at the back waist or on the left shoulder.
- The choice rules and the server's refusals follow v197 (no cap sleeve or cowl on a strapless bodice, a sweep train and soft tulle at most on a sheath or mermaid, no pleats on a sheath, leaving a ball gown resets the petticoat). A fresh gown starts boned, with a waist stay. Every fixture is re-captured from v197.
- Dev lab only (`/dev/gown-maker-lab`); nothing a person sees yet.
