Who you see

Which guests are in the list

+1s
Only seats a guest brings
Shown only when your list has +1s. "No name yet" is a seat still waiting for its guest's name. A new Group by clears this.

Do more

  • Each set of choices is ONE dropdown (Show · +1s · Group by · Sort) — tap one to open its list; a picked one reads in terracotta.
  • Frames only on things you press; topics are split by a divider.
  • Title + one line; the rest is behind ⓘ.
  • The sheet rises from the bottom — every control is in thumb reach; the count of applied choices sits in the title, Clear all and Done at the foot.
  • Select, Excel and the two add ways stay — as the "Do more" actions, not mixed into the choices.
