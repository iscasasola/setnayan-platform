### Part 3 · Attire picks by style family

`figureBySubtype` becomes: per role, prefer the figure whose `style_theme` equals the couple's
resolved style family AND has a colour range; else the first figure with a range (today). Use the

**Guards:** a `bridgerton · regal` couple gets the bridgerton bride, groom, ninang, ninong…; a
couple with no family gets today's pick; a family whose figure lost its range (the MB23 bride
case) falls through to another variant with one. Sabotage: ignore the family → red; prefer the
