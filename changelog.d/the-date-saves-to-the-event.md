## 2026-11-05 · fix(date): the event date saves to the event at once — never through the Event Hub's Apply

Owner, 5 Nov 2026, verbatim: *"it shouldn't rely on the hub."* Measured that day on the demo event: Sat 18 Dec 2027 picked in the Suppliers page's "Your date" sheet showed in the sheet, but `events.event_date` stayed NULL (precision `year`), Home kept saying "No date yet", and nothing said an Apply was needed — the pick had gone into the Event Hub DRAFT (`hubDraftAction` intent `save`). The date drives suppliers, booking clashes, Home, Papic and Live Watch; it is a fact of the event, not a Hub design edit. Amends answer 7 of 2026-10-01 ("EVERYTHING WAITS FOR APPLY") for the date only.

- **One door, the shipped one.** `updateEventDate` (`app/dashboard/[eventId]/actions.ts`) already wrote `events.event_date` directly (Event Settings › Personalization, Explore's "Lock this day"). Every place a couple sets the date now posts to it: the Suppliers page's "Your date" sheet (also opened from Home's Countdown), the Maker's Details › Your date (a day, a month, Help me choose's "Use <day>"), and Home's date-change card once every supplier answered ("Every supplier answered — use <date>").
- **Its checks are the shipped ones, moved — not re-invented.** The date floor (`eventDateFloorRefusal`), the clash refusal at the pick with who (`datePickClash` → `clashStillOpen`, now FAIL-CLOSED since nothing stands behind the pick), `eventDateRefusal` with the booked suppliers' clearance (`dateApplyClearance`, Q8), then the write through the couple's own session (RLS; a write that reached no row is said, not answered "saved"), each booked supplier told "The date moved to …" (`afterDateApplied`), the whole Schedule following the day (`moveScheduleWithDate`), and the dashboard + the published Event Hub drawn again (`revalidateGuestSite` — the Hub reads `events.event_date`; it keeps no copy). Every refusal is returned in words, never thrown. A booked supplier's day moving is now governed by "the supplier in conflict decides" (the clearance) instead of the old blanket "Contact support" lock on Personalization.
- **The draft can no longer hold a date.** `event_date` · `event_date_precision` left `HUB_DRAFT_FACT_COLUMNS`, so `sanitizeState` drops a date from every save, from an older stored draft and from its Undo history — never laid over the preview, counted by ✓ Apply or written by Apply. A save that still carries one (an old screen) is refused in words (`HUB_DRAFT_DATE_IS_LIVE_MESSAGE`). Apply's date steps (`dateHeld`, `dateMove`, the three `date_*` hold reasons) are gone; it still reads the live date (`HUB_LIVE_DATE_COLUMNS`) to stand a drafted ceremony time on the day.
- **The date-change flow no longer writes the draft.** `enterReadyDateIntoDraft` / `takeAskedDateOutOfDraft` are removed; the couple's notice reads "Every supplier has answered — use <date> on Home and it saves at once."
- **Words.** The note after a save: "Saved — this is your date now, everywhere: Home, your suppliers and your Event Hub." (booked: "Saved — your booked suppliers can do this day, and each of them is told it moved."). The Suppliers line's "in your draft" is gone, and the page plans on the event's date (one less read: no draft read).
- **A save never resets the screen.** The sheet's doors are keyed on the priced ids, so the redraw a saved date brings keeps the sheet's own read (Help me choose stays on its month); a date save owes no second redraw at close.
- **Still waits for Apply, unchanged:** the place / venues sheet, the names, the guest-facing words and the looks.
- Tests: new guard `lib/the-date-saves-to-the-event.test.ts`; `a-typed-name-and-date-wait-for-apply.db.test.ts` gains 2b (a stale drafted date is never read or planned) and 2c (the couple's session writes the date at once; a guest's reaches no row); the clash/floor/Schedule tests are re-pointed at the door. Ugat screens map regenerated.
