Suppliers Find: the header and looks from suppliers

Two changes on the shipped Find page. The data is the same and the pieces that already work stay. Tap anything with a dotted underline, the dropdown, the ⚙ and the hearts.

1 · The header

One block at the top, one row when you scroll

  • Expanded: the date is the headline, with the days left beside it. The venue sits under it. Each still opens its own sheet.
  • Compressed: the same row stays pinned. The date and the venue slide into the space beside the dropdown, so you never lose them while scrolling.
  • ⚙ is a plain 40 px icon, no longer a circle bigger than the dropdown.
  • The Setnayan AI line is gone from the page. It was a premium-tier badge from July. Since AI is free for every event in launch, it said nothing a couple can act on. It now lives as one row in ⚙.
  • Build and Booked keep their count in the row (try the dropdown).

2 · Inspiration

Each category shows its suppliers' looks

  • Reception opens on looks its suppliers shared, filtered by part. These are the supplier mood-board photos the couple could only reach today by opening the board, then a part, then tapping its slot.
  • ♡ saves a look to the couple's board under that part. It is the same save the gallery picker already does.
  • "Your inspiration board" becomes "Saved looks", a short row that opens what they kept.
  • "From a real wedding" marks a photo from an event the shop was booked for. "On your list" marks a supplier the couple already picked.
  • Their own photo is a small link, no longer the big ＋ tile.
  • With no shared looks yet, the strip says so in words instead of showing an empty ＋.
