## 2026-08-24 · feat(privacy): the two adopted deletion periods are enforced — face data at 3 months, a supplier's raw identity uploads at 90 days

Two retention promises were adopted by the owner as DPO on 2026-08-17, written into the NPC pack, and **nothing ran them**. The pack says so itself, twice, in the rows that state the periods: *"ADOPTED 2026-08-17, ENFORCEMENT NOT YET BUILT. No sweep implements this period yet."* A retention period nothing enforces is the promise a filing is judged on.

`lib/retention-sweep.ts` was NOT this (chat only, 5-year, via `purge_expired_chat`) and neither was `lib/vendor-dossier-retention.ts` (Deep Search web-research data — transient and regenerable, not identity documents). Both were copied for SHAPE: a weekly `claimPeriodicJob` compare-and-swap driven cron-free from admin request traffic, best-effort, never throws.

**1 · Guest face data, three months after the event ENDS** — `lib/face-data-retention{,-core}.ts`.

The clock is `GREATEST(COALESCE(event_end_date, event_date), event_date)` plus the grace period — reproduced exactly from `papic_events_past_fullres_clock`, because the pack says this is *"the same clock as the full-resolution photo floor"*. `FACE_DATA_POST_EVENT_GRACE_DAYS` is therefore an **import of `FULL_RES_POST_EVENT_GRACE_DAYS` (92), not a second copy of 92** — a re-typed constant is how one rule becomes two that drift. 92 is the longest three-calendar-month span, so "three months" is true for every event date rather than most.

Three things go together, because the pack scopes the row across both stores — *"Singapore (Supabase — the vectors) + APAC (R2 — the source selfie images)"*: the face vector, the enrollment row, and the source selfie object.

🔑 **AND THE SELFIE IS ALSO THE GUEST'S AVATAR.** `app/[slug]/actions.ts` writes ONE ref to TWO columns — `guests.photo_url` (line ~341) and `guest_face_enrollments.asset_url` (line ~410). Deleting the object without clearing the avatar leaves a broken picture on the guest's own invitation page; clearing the avatar without deleting the object retains the biometric source. `purgeUserGuestBiometrics` already names this exact trap. Both move together, object first, and the avatar is cleared **only** where it is byte-identical to that ref — a `couple_upload` or `oauth_google` avatar is a different object and is untouched.

⛔ **NOT touched, deliberately:** `user_face_profiles` (ACCOUNT-scoped, one row per user, dies with the account — an event clock has no authority over it); photos and tags (verified in the live schema: **zero** foreign keys reference `guest_face_enrollments`, so the delete is a leaf and an `auto_face` tag carries the guest link itself). The face data expires; the memories it helped find do not.

**2 · A supplier's raw identity uploads, 90 days after the decision** — `lib/vendor-identity-retention{,-core}.ts`.

Deletes exactly the pack's "raw uploads" list — government ID, selfie/liveness, bank micro-deposit, portfolio — from `vendor_verification_applications.doc_uploads` and the identity key columns on `vendor_verifications`, plus the R2 objects behind them. **DTI / BIR 2303 / Mayor's Permit are retained SEVEN YEARS and are copied through untouched**, as is the decision record. The two slot lists are asserted disjoint in tests, because a slot drifting between them would delete a document we told the regulator we keep. Slots are **removed, not blanked** — a key left present still reads as "this document was collected".

`/admin/verification-docs` is the opposite of this job and was not extended: it deletes only objects that are provably UNREFERENCED, one at a time, after a person looked at each. This retires files that are still referenced, then clears the reference.

**🚨 REPORTED, NOT FIXED — the pack over-declares collection we do not do.** The owner pruned the slot list on 2026-07-03 (*"we do not need this … what we have, that is it"*): `government_id`, `live_selfie`, `phone_email_otp` and `amlc_screening` are RETIRED and identity confirmation is the 15-minute Google Meet. The pack was regenerated a **month later** and still declares a government ID, a liveness video and AMLC screening, and still names Persona · Veriff · Onfido · ComplyAdvantage as recipients. This is the opposite direction of the retention defect and still wrong: it tells a regulator we run a screening programme that does not exist. It is corpus + DPO territory, so it is surfaced rather than edited. The retired slots ARE still swept — the pruning comment says stored values are *"simply ignored"*, and ignored is not deleted.

**Posture — over-deleting is worse than the gap, and there is no inverse.** R2 is not versioned and a vector cannot be recomputed from a deleted image. Every branch fails CLOSED: an unreadable event date, a decision that never happened, a failed read or an unparseable ref SKIPS the row. `GREATEST` is a one-way valve — a malformed end date earlier than the start can only ever be ignored, never pull the clock backwards. Switching either sweep off makes it a **DRY RUN, not a no-op**, because a disabled job that reports nothing is indistinguishable from a broken one.

Safe by arithmetic at merge, not by optimism: production holds **0** face enrollments, **0** account face profiles, **0** `vendor_verifications` rows and **1** verification application with no decision and no identity uploads. Nothing is eligible on day one, which is the right moment to ship irreversible deletion.
