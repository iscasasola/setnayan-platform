## 2026-11-28 · feat(social): Facebook posts itself, in the owner's three slots

Owner, 28 Nov (specs DECISION_LOG "FACEBOOK POSTING IS AUTOMATIC, IN THREE DAILY SLOTS", "WHAT EACH FACEBOOK SLOT CARRIES", "THE FACEBOOK DAILY CAP COUNTS THE PLANNED POSTS TOO"): posting is automatic at 8 AM, 2 PM and 8 PM Manila, with holiday greetings as an extra. Server side only; no page changes.

- **Slots (`lib/social/governor.ts`, rewritten):** the slots are 08:00 morning, 14:00 afternoon and 20:00 evening, plus 12:00 holiday, which opens only for a planned row marked holiday. They replace the 12–1 PM and 7–9 PM windows and the weekend 10 AM window. One post per slot instant is the whole daily cap, and planned posts count toward it. Every site auto-post (new shops, badges, recaps, milestones, teasers, announcements) takes the next free 14:00. A slot stays open for its hour. A missed post is re-slotted (see below). When two rows hold one slot, the planned post keeps it.
- **2 PM live composer (`lib/social/live-update.ts` + `.server.ts`):** from 10:00 to 13:50 (13:50 is the last moment Facebook still takes it as a scheduled 2 PM post), if today's 14:00 is still empty, it reads this week's real news and composes one post. On Sunday that is the weekly totals; otherwise new verified suppliers (named only on a paid size, never counted), then events held this week. Counts are round totals from the milestone ladder (10 and up) only. With no real news, the slot stays empty and the server log says so once that day. One live update a day (`source_ref = live:<day>`); a pulled one is never re-drafted.
- **Library import (`apps/web/scripts/social/import-library.ts` + `lib/social/library-import.ts`):** reads the library v2 CSV and writes an idempotent migration. Each row becomes `source_type 'library'` with `source_ref` = a hash of date + slot, the caption as body, the Door as `link_url`, the pinned comment as `first_comment`, and its slot and Manila time stored as UTC. A row already present in any status is skipped. Bad rows stop the run with their line number. The real migration is not generated here; the controller generates it once library v2 merges.
- **First comment (`lib/social/facebook.ts` `commentOnFacebookPost`):** right after a Facebook post goes out, its `first_comment` is posted under it. The Graph API has no call to pin a comment, so `platform_results.facebook.first_comment` records the comment and `pin: "pin by hand"`.
- **Multi-photo posts (controller, 28 Nov; `lib/social/facebook-multi-photo.ts`):** a row with 2 or more photos in `media_urls` becomes ONE Facebook post. Each photo uploads to `/{page}/photos` with `published=false`, in order, then `/{page}/feed` attaches them in that order (`attached_media`). If any upload or the feed post fails, nothing is posted, the error is recorded, and the unpublished uploads are deleted. The single-photo and link paths are unchanged. Instagram and TikTok take the first photo (`media_url`). The importer fills `media_urls` from "Images, in this order". Relative paths resolve under `--images-base` plus the row's Folder; without a base, the run stops.
- **Facebook holds the schedule (owner ruling, 28 Nov: "schedule posts on Facebook itself and stay cron-free"):** there is no timer, no workflow and no secret. On any site visit, the ordinary `after()` flush hands every slot post for the next ~7 days to Facebook as a scheduled post (`published=false` + `scheduled_publish_time`, 10 minutes to 30 days ahead), and Facebook publishes it at the slot (`lib/social/fb-queue.ts`, `lib/social/facebook-request.ts`). Multi-photo posts are scheduled too: photos upload unpublished and temporary, then one scheduled `/feed` post attaches them, still at most 10 and all or nothing.
  - Each row stores Facebook's id (`facebook_post_id`) and shows the status `scheduled_on_fb`.
  - Pulling the post deletes it on Facebook first, and editing it updates it there first. If Facebook refuses, nothing changes and the reason is shown.
  - Turning autopublish or Facebook off takes back every post still waiting on Facebook. Saving the switches runs the flush at once.
  - The first comment cannot be scheduled, so the first visit after the slot confirms the post went out, adds the comment, marks the row published, and runs Instagram and TikTok if the slot is still open.
  - A post that cannot be handed over in time (composed less than 10 minutes before its slot) still goes out on the first visit inside its slot hour.
  - Claim-first updates mean two overlapping visits never schedule a post twice or comment twice.
- **A hand-off is never lost or doubled (controller review, 28 Nov):**
  - A schedule call that fails or times out may still have been accepted, so the Page's scheduled posts (`GET /{page-id}/scheduled_posts`) are read before any retry. A post already there is adopted with its id and never scheduled again. It is matched by its exact slot second plus its own text, and two matches are never guessed between.
  - A claimed hand-off whose id was never saved (a failed save, or `after()` cut off) is reconciled after 10 minutes (`recoverStuckHandOffs`). Found → it gets its id. Not there with its slot ahead → back to the queue. Not there after its slot, or ambiguous → failed with "check the Page".
  - Pull works on such a post: it is found on the Page and deleted there first. The queue page keeps its Pull reachable.
  - A couple's take-down deletes on Facebook before it pulls, for a post on Facebook's schedule or a cut-off hand-off (`takeDownRevoked`). A row whose Facebook side is unclear keeps its slot until it is clear. A post being sent right now is left to the manual take-down lane.
  - At most 5 posts are handed over per flush, inside 60 seconds.
  - A Pull or take-down never meets a hand-off in flight: one claimed under 10 minutes ago is refused with "Being handed to Facebook — try again in a few minutes". If Facebook still takes a post the row no longer holds, the post is deleted on Facebook at once. If that delete fails, the row is marked `takedown_needed` for the manual take-down lane.
  - Every scheduled photo post, one photo too, goes as unpublished `/photos` uploads plus one scheduled `/feed` post, so `/scheduled_posts` lists it with its text.
  - A Page read that left posts unread is not proof of absence: such a row goes to "check the Page" and is never requeued.
  - The 60-second budget also bounds one post: past it, no further Graph call starts for that post. If no call was made yet, it goes back to the queue; otherwise it waits for recovery.
- **A missed planned post is moved, never lost:** it goes to the next free slot of its own kind (a missed 8 AM moves to the next free 8 AM), is logged, and is recorded in `platform_results.missed_slots`. Only a holiday greeting expires, because it is tied to its date.
- **One post per slot is a database rule (controller re-review):** the migration adds the partial UNIQUE index `social_posts_one_post_per_slot` on `scheduled_for`, covering slotted rows that are scheduled, publishing or published. Each slot kind has its own hour, so one instant is one Manila day and slot. Two overlapping flushes can no longer put two posts in one slot: the second write is refused (23505), and `claimFreeSlot` reads that as "slot taken" and moves to the next free one. The library-import SQL now gives a planned slot to the plan: it moves an unsent auto-post off it, and skips a planned slot that an already-sent post holds, instead of aborting.
- **Review fixes:** at most 10 photos per post (the owner's rule; 11 is refused before anything uploads). A failed delete of an unpublished photo is logged and recorded in `platform_results.facebook.cleanup_failures`. The importer refuses impossible dates such as 2026-02-30, which `Date` used to roll over to 2 March. The Graph token never travels in a URL: it goes in the request body for POST and DELETE, and in the Authorization header for the page-token exchange.
- **Flush (`lib/social/flush.ts`):** a slot pass (re-slot, expire, settle clashes), then the 14:00 assignment, then the live composer. Dispatch publishes only a row inside its slot hour. `autopublish_enabled` and the per-platform switches still gate every publish.
- **Admin queue actions (`app/admin/social-queue/actions.ts`):** Post now uses the slot open right now, else the next free 2 PM. Retry hands the post back to the governor. Announcements take the next free 2 PM. No new server action.
- **The queue page lists posts waiting on Facebook (`app/admin/studio/_surfaces/social-queue-surface.tsx`, #8437 now on main):** its queue read takes `scheduled_on_fb` too, so a post handed to Facebook stays on the page with its Pull and Edit copy controls. "Post now" on such a post is refused as not schedulable, since Facebook already holds its time.
