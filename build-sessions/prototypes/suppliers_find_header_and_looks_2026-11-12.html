1 · The header

Expanded at the top, two rows when pinned

  • Expanded: the date and the place, then the shipped dropdown row (Find · Build · Booked, its count, ⚙), then the Setnayan AI line with a switch.
  • Compressed, two rows: row 1 holds the date and place in short form plus Setnayan AI with its switch. Row 2 is the shipped dropdown row, the same at rest and pinned.
  • The switch is new in this spot. It writes the same setting as Event settings › Plan it myself (events.planning_mode), reversed: AI on = Plan it myself off.
  • Lines when on: Smart matching, fit scoring, and the watch guard are on — free while we are in launch. When off, the Plan it myself list: Supplier matching Off · Deadlines Off · Reminders Off.
  • ⚙ is a 40 px icon instead of a circle bigger than the dropdown. It and the date and place still open their shipped sheets.
  • Who has AI: Event Plus and Event Pro turn it on (activateEventPlan, lib/sku-activation.ts). The paywall is parked today, so every event has it, Free included. With the paywall on, a Free event has no switch. It gets the shipped line Setnayan AI comes with Event Plus and Event Pro and Unlock instead.
