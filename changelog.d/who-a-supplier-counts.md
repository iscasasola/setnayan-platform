A flat-priced service takes no tick and no rule, is never listed, gets no tile, and an old tick on one leaves nobody out.
- **Not counted for ▾ lists every supplier on the event, not only booked ones** (any status, not archived), with the hint "<shop> · Booked" / "<shop> · Quoted · applies once booked", and the line under it: "<shop> counts N, not N+1 · Every other service still counts <first name>." The trigger keeps its same-event check; the readers still count booked suppliers only, so a tick on a quote changes nothing until it is booked.
- **"Who they count", a rule per service** — new table `event_vendor_count_rules` (mode `all` | `only` | `except`, roles, guest-group ids; RLS at CREATE TABLE, couple + admin; a deleted group drops out by trigger).
