## 2026-11-01 · feat(schedule): chapters — a chapter holds moments, a moment holds parallel tasks; make them in Home › Event Settings

Step 2 of `SCHEDULE_CHAPTERS_BUILD_PLAN_2026-11-01.md` (§2, §3, §3b, §3c, §7). Extends the schedule, no chapter table.

- **Schema** (`20271300072429_a_chapter_holds_moments_and_parallel_tasks.sql`): a chapter is a top-level `event_schedule_blocks` row, a moment its child. New block columns: `spot` (Exact spot / a moment's room), `ingress_minutes_before` / `egress_minutes_after` (minutes from the start, so they move with the chapter and cross midnight; NULL = none), `call_lead_minutes` (0/15/30/45/60, default 60), `on_invitation` (default off), `photo_who` / `photo_when`, and for moments `is_optional` and `travel_from` / `travel_to`. New table `event_schedule_tasks`: one row per person, supplier (+ optional crew), group, role or named person, with what they do, an optional call time and an optional place (NULL = the moment's room). RLS at CREATE TABLE: couple + schedule-Edit helpers write, View helpers and booked suppliers read (not staged blocks), guests and anon nothing (no anon grant). `event_id` is derived from the block; a who on another event is refused. `move_schedule_chapter` (SECURITY INVOKER) moves a chapter and its moments in one statement.
- **`event_parts` is not needed** and was never built (0 migrations, 0 code references on `origin/main`); chapters-as-blocks replace it.
- **Event Settings › Chapters** (`event-settings-chapters.tsx`): hosts, and a helper with Edit on the Schedule, add a chapter with name + date + start, then open it for Where (or Venue TBA), Exact spot, Ends, Ingress from, Egress until, Call time, Who sees the photos, When, On the invitation, Notes; Delete warns → Continue → Deleting. Every save goes through the Schedule's own `createScheduleBlock` / `updateScheduleBlock` / `deleteScheduleBlock` (no new server action — the route budget is at its ceiling). Dates stop at the event day. No guest-visible change: chapters made here start hidden from guests.
- `updateScheduleBlock` and `deleteScheduleBlock` now read back what they changed and say "nothing changed" on a zero-row answer (RLS refusal) instead of a quiet success. `updateScheduleBlock` accepts the new fields, `with_moments=1` moves the moments with a new start, and putting a chapter on the invitation is the hosts' and coordinator's call (`mayShowToGuests`).
- New shared `TimeRow` (`app/_components/form-row-time.tsx`) beside `DateRow`.
- Pure helpers `lib/schedule-chapters.ts` (ingress/egress/end across midnight, date cap, task place, clash = same person or crew in two places at once — found, never refused) and server helpers `lib/schedule-chapters.server.ts` (read chapters, read/add/update/delete tasks with read-back). Task screens are Step 3.
- Labs: `/dev/chapters-lab` (create/edit/delete on fake chapters; one moment with three parallel tasks in two places; `?clash=1`), and `/dev/home-lab?settings=1&row=chapters`.
