## 2026-10-20 · fix(og): the link preview wears the couple's own logo

Owner: "the link sent did not use the logo of cale-ice on the thumbnail". Measured on production: `/cale-ice` published `og:image = /api/og/realstory-slug/cale-ice?v=…`, and that picture drew the typed initials "IC". The couple's cover and QR centre draw the I&C logo they made. The route's events read never selected the logo columns.

- `app/api/og/realstory-slug/[slug]/route.ts` now reads the mark through `HERO_MONOGRAM_COLUMNS` plus `role_palette` and `logo_wanted`, and resolves it with the cover's own `heroMarkSvg(event)`. That one call handles the Logo switch (DECISION_LOG 2026-10-15 (4)), the read-time SVG safety gate, centring on the ink and the mood-board repaint. Both monogram cards (the invitation card and a chosen monogram cover) receive it. The logo is resolved only after the existing visibility gate, so the card shows strangers nothing the cover does not.
- New `lib/social/card-logo.ts`: rasterises the resolved mark to an inert PNG (trimmed to its ink, fitted into 480×210, 4 s limit, blank-raster check) and builds the satori `<img>` node with `src` as a top-level prop (the `vendor-card` outage shape). Any failure returns null, and the card draws the initials exactly as before. Switched-off and no-logo cards are byte-identical to the old renderer's output.
- `lib/social/realstory-card.tsx` `monogramCardTree`: the logo takes the initials' place. The layout, header, names, date and wordmark are unchanged. This is the existing cream monogram card: no OG variant of the cover's palette/background exists, so none was invented.
- Cache: `ogCardUrlFor` takes a `look` token (`lib/social/share-card-look.ts` `shareCardLookToken`, an FNV hash of the drawn mark or of the initials and colour, plus the edition `logo1`). `/[slug]` metadata passes it, so the address changes now and again whenever the logo or the switch changes. Posts that were already shared keep their cached preview. Facebook's Sharing Debugger can re-scrape them.
- Guard `lib/social/the-link-preview-wears-the-logo.test.ts`. It renders through satori and sharp to check pixels: a logo that is set and wanted is drawn; a logo that is off, unset, unsafe, blank, broken or too slow is not, so the card falls back to initials. It also pins the route and card wiring and the address token. The census in `lib/logo-switch-is-real.test.ts` now lists the OG route (carries) and `app/[slug]/page.tsx` (via loaders). `a-sealed-celebration-looks-missing.test.ts` accepts the template form of the select.
