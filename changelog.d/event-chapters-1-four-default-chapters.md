## 2026-11-03 · feat(schedule): event chapters slice 1 — four default public chapters on every new wedding, swipe left to delete with Undo

Owner-approved 3–4 Nov 2026 (DECISION_LOG "EVERY EVENT IS BUILT FROM PREBUILT PARTS", "ALL FOUR PREBUILT PARTS ARE DEFAULT PUBLIC CHAPTERS", "SWIPE LEFT TO DELETE ON EVERY LIST ROW"; approved prototype "Event chapters — 4 Nov 2026", frames 1, 3, 4, 10).

- **Four default chapters**: a NEW wedding's Schedule starts with Prep 7–12 · Ceremony 1:30–3:30 · Cocktail + Reception 5–10 · After Party 10:30 onward (the prototype's hours), every one public, with the prototype's tip line while they are still unfilled. The moments inside are the shipped rite-aware template (`buildScheduleSeed`), reshaped by `buildDefaultChapters` in `lib/schedule.ts` — never a second list. A dance-free rite keeps the shipped rule (no cocktail hour, no After Party → Prep · Ceremony · Reception).
- **Which events are new**: migration `20271306532960` adds the service-only `event_default_chapters_pending` marker, left by a trigger on every `events` INSERT (every creation path, one mechanism). The app's fail-soft first-open seed `seedDefaultChapters` (`lib/schedule-seed.server.ts`, beside the shipped non-wedding seed) claims it once and writes the chapters when a host (or an Edit delegate) first opens the Schedule. With no exact day yet they go on the seed's placeholder day, stamped on the marker, and a trigger moves them (with their moments) onto the real day the moment any path sets one — a failed move never refuses the date. Events made before this ship have no marker and are never touched; a host who deletes all four never gets them back.
- **Swipe left to delete**: the guest rows' retired `SwipeToDelete` gesture is recovered as ONE shared part (`app/_components/swipe-to-delete.tsx`) and used on every chapter on the Event Day rail and on the Time TBA list. The swipe only asks — the same warning words as Delete chapter (`ChapterDeleteWords`), which now name the suppliers taken off their parts and say when no photos move.
- **Undo**: a deleted chapter is gone at once with "<chapter> deleted · Undo" (the shared `PeekToast`); the one removal is sent when the undo window closes or the page is left, so Undo writes nothing and loses nothing. Delete chapter in the chapter's sheet goes through the same window. The warning no longer says "cannot be undone".
- **Chapters may overlap**: pinned by a guard — nothing warns between two chapters; a flag is only the same person in two places.
