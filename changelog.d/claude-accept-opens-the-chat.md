## 2026-11-11 · fix(supplier): every Clients row and every supplier notice open the picked client; Accept lands in the chat

Owner, 11 Nov, live on his phone as SetnaProd, on a new request ("TEST SetnaProd walk · Birthday · Asks for Mobile bar + Photo Booth"): *"Why do i see it this way? The chatbox is not yet done?"* · *"Accepted it and still did not show the chatbox"* · then, verbatim: *"We had the clients> that show calendar on the page and the list is at the bottom. When that is pressed it jump to the supplie-client card that was already planned with the details-chat-form-quote-files-report and more menus that would grow to full screen"*.

**Cause.** The sheet he saw was `RosterRowView` (Clients › Asking) risen by Build 5 slice 2 into its OWN `RiseScreen`: the event's lines and `InquiryAnswers` (Decline · Ask a question · Accept), with no page menu and no chat. The v118 #38 landing (`clientLandingPage` / `openClientHref`) had shipped, but only the row's door button used it, and an asking row had no door. Accept posted `return_to = acceptTo ?? returnTo` with `returnTo="/vendor-dashboard/customers"`, so after saying yes the supplier landed back on the list and the card.

**Fix (Rule 0 — shipped parts only).**
- `SupplierOpenRow` gains `opensTo`: below 701 the row's head rises INTO that page (the picked client, from the tapped row via `rememberPickedOrigin`) instead of its own body. From 701 it keeps its right column (the wide Rise is still Lane R's owner pick).
- `RosterRowView` passes `opensTo={href}` (`hrefFor`, the one open path) for EVERY row: an ask opens the picked client on Chat, where the facts and the three answers are the chat's shipped top band (`data-chat-inquiry-card`); booked rows open on Details. The roster no longer draws `InquiryAnswers`, and the `answers` plumbing is gone from `customers/page.tsx`.
- `InquiryAnswers`: Accept lands in the chat, never on `returnTo`. The full-screen chat still opens the quote (`?compose=quote`). Inside the picked client (`chatHref`), Ask and Accept stay on its own Chat page. Decline is unchanged.
- **Supplier notices** (from the coordinator's audit of origin/main):
  1. Inquiry, message, meeting, quote answer, "marked you as booked", cancelled, payment logged, and incoming call now open `supplierChatHref(eventId, threadId)`, the picked client's Chat page. Before, they opened the bare `/vendor-dashboard/messages/<thread>`. The emitters are `lib/chat-actions.ts`, `app/_components/negotiation-actions.ts`, `app/vendor-dashboard/proposals/actions.ts`, `app/dashboard/[eventId]/vendors/actions.ts`, `app/dashboard/[eventId]/budget/actions.ts` and `app/_actions/thread-call-actions.ts`. `inquiry_displaced` stays on the thread page, with its reason: a released inquiry may no longer open the client, and that page says who closed it. The thread route itself does NOT forward, because the picked client's Chat page renders that same route's body (`SupplierThreadBody`).
  2. `guest_takedown_honored` (`lib/tell-the-supplier-it-came-down.ts`) opens the live console only on the event day. Any other day it opens the picked client's Hub.
  3. `schedule_ask` (`lib/change-the-schedule.server.ts`, the crew skip ask) opens the floor only on the event day. Any other day it opens the picked client's Schedule.

  Both use the new `supplierLiveOrClientHref`, which follows the same rule as the shipped `scheduleShiftHref`.
- Lab: `/dev/clients-lab?v=pick&stage=asking` draws the picked client for a new request (unbooked menu, step 1 Inquiry, the ask on Chat, `&accepted=1` after Accept). The lab's Asking row opens it.
