What this prototype is

The supplier's one quote tool inside a conversation (#build-quote on /vendor-dashboard/messages/[threadId]), redrawn as five steps on one spine. No JavaScript — every state above is a click in the grey bar. Measured before drawing: the live composer on origin/main has 12 buttons, 22 inputs, 6 selects, 1 textarea and 17 blocks in one scroll, ~740 visible words; production holds 2 service cards, 0 packages, 0 templates, 3 threads (all accepted) and 0 quotes sent through this composer — so nothing here can be checked against a live screen, only against this drawing and the executed tests.

What changes, one sentence per screen

  1. Step 1 · Know the event — the rail's rows plus area, asked-for, budget band, style and locked categories sit beside the quote; withheld fields are named only when the fee flag is on. D · held
  2. Step 2 · Choose what to offer — the shop's service cards seed the quote (one or several); add-ons, comes-with, slots, lead-time and capacity ride along; the package picker stays but only shows with packages. A B · held
  3. Step 3 · Set the price — a review of the seeded lines, crew and travel opened by the card's flags, the discount applied with its reason and removable, the booking fee and the maximum Papic deal (shipped copy), and the per-quote gift switch drawn disabled until G. A C · held G · later wave
  4. Step 4 · Terms — the card's payment schedule and reservation terms seed the self-balancing schedule; rails, title, validity, note as shipped. A C · held
  5. Step 5 · Review & send — the card the couple opens, then Send. C · held
  6. The thread — the quote card replaces the offered card and is drawn through the same card face; the superseded offer stays reachable as history. E · held (frame file)

What is deliberately NOT changing

  • The one button on the quote card is still decided by lib/quote-card-state.ts (#5607): Review & accept · View proposal · Update this quote · Counter-offer · the Lock pointer. Superseded and re-accept behave as shipped. No second card state is drawn.
  • The money: lines through resolvePackageLine, the schedule through resolveSchedule, the fee through bookingFeePhp (5% to ₱100,000 then 1%, floor ₱50), the gift through previewGiftForTotal (40% of the fee, cap 50,000, the live ladder). Nothing priced twice.
  • The tools mount only on an accepted thread (toolsMounted); the Accept/Decline block, the offer tool, the call, deal-or-meeting and Log the outcome are untouched.
  • The in-thread Lock stays a pointer to the workspace; nothing lock-shaped was added.
  • The 2026-09-09 gift rules: optional, exactly 40%, no dial, computed from what the supplier pays, the number on the QUOTE never the card.

What this cannot prove

  • Nobody has seen this at 375px or 1440px in a real browser from a session: the Browser pane renders a local file as a static snapshot. The file was read back and grep -c '<script' prints 0. Please open it on your phone.
  • Five thread states have never rendered in production (0 rows). The pending / declined / displaced / withdrawn / expired blocks are drawn from the shipped source; three of them (displaced, withdrawn, expired) have no shipped sentence and print the declined copy today — the amber blocks propose one and ask you to confirm it.
  • The server read (loadServiceCardLinesForQuote) — RLS refusing a competitor's card, the event date read after ownership — is typechecked and read, not executed. A db-test against the PGlite replay is the evidence that would convince me.
  • Every [bracketed] value: inclusions, add-ons, schedule rows, slots, serves, the pay rails, the travel fee, the inquiry count. Prod holds none of those rows yet.

Open questions

  1. Answer 2's loss. Replacing the offered card removes the original offer from the thread. Recommendation: keep it reachable as history (drawn). Cost of getting it wrong: a couple comparing three suppliers loses the pitch they were comparing on, silently.
  2. The three closed-state sentences (displaced · withdrawn · expired). Recommendation: the proposed copy above. Cost: a couple who withdrew reads "You declined this inquiry" on the supplier's screen — a false record of who ended it.
  3. Which card the bill reads for a combined quote before G lands. Recommendation: land G in the next wave and, until then, the Papic row follows the first card (drawn). Cost: a supplier could be billed for a gift the quote did not show, or shown one the bill will not carry.
  4. A card with no price — shown greyed with "finish the card ›" (drawn) or hidden? Recommendation: shown. Cost of hiding: the supplier does not learn why their card is not offered.
  5. Step order 2 ↔ 3 — cards before price is Answer 4; "+ Feature" stays reachable in step 3 for a quote with no card. Confirm.
