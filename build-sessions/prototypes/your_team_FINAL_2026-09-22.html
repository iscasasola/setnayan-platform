In plain words

1 · What changes, one line per screen

  • Find — the bench comes to the top and a supplier becomes their card: price, lock state, next step, actions, all in one place instead of a roster repeating them.
  • Badges — unread rides on the folder, the category and the card, from one stored count. Your idea, and it is what lets the roster go.
  • Money — “paid to suppliers” and “Setnayan orders” stop being one figure, and the buffer refuses to compute while anyone has no price.
  • Overflow — the two strips above the bench get a ceiling of three rows and a door.
  • Plans — unchanged, and it keeps “Compare side by side”.

2 · What is deliberately NOT changing

  • The bench accordion. Folder → category → card carousel is your 2026-06-16 design. It is reproduced, not redrawn.
  • The Messages menu row. Still absent, per your 2026-07-10 ruling.
  • The xl-only conversation column — your 2026-09-08 three-column decision.
  • The counts. Overview keeps its number; this page's is a different noun. No reconciliation.
  • Prices. Nothing here invents one. Every “no price recorded” is a real NULL.

3 · What this prototype cannot prove

  • That the badge counts are right in production. They need notifications.event_id, which merged today (#5857), and a couple with real unread. Production has 3 chat threads and 13 messages, all accepted.
  • That comparison is useful. The marketplace holds one published vendor with two service cards. The venues you can compare here are is_demo = true; zero non-demo directory rows carry a price. Find-and-compare is the right build and it has almost nothing to work on yet.
  • Touch and scroll on your phone. This is a desktop browser drawing; the 3,643-line bench will behave differently under a real finger.
  • The failure state. It does not exist in code. I drew what it should say; nobody has seen it refuse.
  • Any price or fit badge. Fit needs distance, allotment and calendar reads that only the server has.

  1. May this page go to code at all? Recommend yes, in the slice order below. Cost of being wrong: I build a page you did not want, and the controller's hold existed precisely to stop that.
  2. The money split. Recommend yes. Cost of being wrong: a couple reads “₱2,250,000 to spare” while two venues are locked and ₱26,499 has moved — which is what happens today.
