## 2026-11-04 · feat(schedule): event chapters slice 3 — venue hours: the venue sets a chapter's hours, the host approves, then they are held

Owner-approved 4 Nov 2026 (approved prototype "Event chapters — 4 Nov 2026", frames 2 and 7b: *"The venue that mainly controls the chapter keeps its hours 🔒; the host asks it to change"* · *"The 🔒 only appears once a venue is booked to the chapter"*). Extends the shipped request/lock machinery — no second mechanism.

- **A new request kind, `hours`** (migration `20271307658341`) on `event_schedule_suggestions`, with `proposed_ingress_minutes` / `proposed_egress_minutes`: a VENUE booked to a top-level chapter (RESTRICTIVE policy via `current_vendor_is_chapter_venue`) proposes Starts · Ends · Setup from · Pack-up until from its schedule ("Set hours", `supplier-schedule.tsx`; `suggestScheduleChange` `request = 'hours'`).
- **The host approves it** in the shipped Requests sheet (worded "the chapter's hours"); the one accept path (`decideSuggestion`) calls `apply_venue_hours`, one SECURITY DEFINER door that writes the hours and sets `event_schedule_blocks.hours_vendor_id`. Auto-agree applies it the same way.
- **Held hours are locked to the host** (`event_schedule_blocks_venue_hours_lock`): a change to a held chapter's start, end, Time TBA, ingress or egress is refused with "<venue> holds this chapter's hours. Ask them to change." The event's date moving (by the anchor, or a new event's placeholder day) and Running late still move it. Taking the venue off the chapter lifts the hold. The supplier part lock gets the matching pass.
- **Ask <venue> to change**: a held chapter's page shows "Hours from <venue> 🔒", its hours line and the shipped host ask (`AskSupplierSection`, new `askLabel`); the venue's Accept goes through `apply_venue_hours`. The chapter sheet shows held hours as one fact row instead of fields.
- With no venue holding a chapter, the host sets and adjusts its hours exactly as before.
