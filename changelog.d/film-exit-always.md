## 2026-08-04 · fix(save-the-date): a way out of the film, at any point

The cinematic opening had one door, and it was at the very end.

A guest who lifted the veil, watched a beat or two and then wanted the actual
invitation page — the schedule, the dress code, the seat — had no way to get
there. The only "See our page" button lived on the film's final beat, so the
website was reachable only by sitting through the whole film. Meanwhile the
veil, correctly, stays up for as long as the film is the experience — so its
petals kept falling over everything, which is what the owner saw on his phone
three days running.

A quiet "See our page ↓" now sits in the corner for the whole film, opposite the
sound control and deliberately the same weight as it, so it reads as a way out
rather than as a competing invitation. It appears only once the veil has been
lifted and the film has actually begun — before that it does not exist at all,
so nobody can tab straight past the opening the couple paid for. The closing
beat keeps its full button, and "watch our film again" still brings the whole
opening back.

SPEC IMPACT: None — the two 2026-06 rulings (the veil is a persistent top layer;
the film and the veil are both reachable) are unchanged. This adds the exit that
was always implied by open browse.
