## 2026-11-07 · feat(suppliers): when the couple approves a supplier who joined from their link, their list takes the supplier's contact corrections

Owner, 7 Nov 2026: *"changing name or number, address, just gets notified."* · *"just need approval for the purchase."*

- **On Approve** (`answerClaimAccess`, `app/dashboard/[eventId]/access-requests/actions.ts`), the couple's own record (the row + their hand-typed card) is compared with the shop the supplier corrected at the claim door (`vendor_profiles`, whichever door wrote it). The difference is kept on the answered ask, `event_access_requests.decisions.updated`, and every couple member gets one in-app notice (`vendor_joined`, app-only, nothing added to the email allowlist). The rule is `lib/supplier-corrections.ts`.
- **The Suppliers page** reads the couple's answered claim asks on their own session (`event_access_requests_host_read` is couple-scoped). For the row an approved ask points at, still bonded to the same shop, the couple sees the shop's **real name and logo**, even while the shop is anonymous to everyone else. The Booked row's second line says "updated by the supplier", and the opened row has an ⓘ, e.g. "Name updated by the supplier", that says what changed.
- **The new phone and address are shown in full, only to the inviting couple.** Owner, 7 Nov 2026: *"we are giving them more freedom to talk outside since we already tracked them from our website that they have been discovered by others. we allow their calling on the app and video calls on the app. that is already built. but only for booked."* The ⓘ and the notice read, for example, "Phone: +63 918 111 2222 (was +63 917 000 0000)" and "Address: 18 Mabini St, Marikina (was Quezon City)". The values travel on the same couple-scoped ask as the real name, so another couple, a guest and anon see nothing. `lib/no-door-out-of-the-app.test.ts` lets this one surface through by name (`SUPPLIER_CORRECTIONS_SURFACE`, quoting the ruling). Its two new checks fail if any other couple-facing or public file reads the corrections, if the page feeds them from anywhere but the couple's own read, or if the number rides anywhere on the Booked row except the ⓘ. Rules 1–6 are unchanged for every other surface.
- **Price and services are never taken.** Nothing is written to `event_vendors` or `event_manual_vendors`, so the couple keeps their own price and services. A change to what they bought reaches them only as an amendment through the shipped flow. Extra service cards a supplier adds to their own shop do not touch the couple.
- **No schema change.** The ask row already stores this, and its RLS is the "revealed to this couple" rule.
