## 2026-09-27 · fix(maker): the Event Hub draft always fits its row, and a failed save never crashes the Maker

Prod, the owner editing his own wedding's Event Hub: the draft row's CHECK caps it at 200,000 bytes, and Undo's history was capped only by COUNT — each entry a FULL state, ~24 KB per Logo edit (traced layers + the composed SVG). Seven Logo edits filled 168 KB of history, the next save hit the cap, and so did every save after it. The element sheet said "Something went wrong"; a plain form save to the draft threw into the Maker's POST and rendered a full 500 page. After a clean Apply, the ⋯ panel stayed open over the Maker.

- `lib/hub-draft.ts` — `HUB_DRAFT_BYTE_BUDGET` (180,000, under the 200,000 CHECK). `hubDraftBytes` counts the way Postgres counts `octet_length(jsonb::text)` (UTF-8 bytes plus jsonb's spaces after `:` and `,`; checked against PGlite). `fitHubDraftHistory` drops the OLDEST Undo states until the draft fits — run by `mergeHubDraft`, `sanitizeHubDraft` and the store's one write door. `hubDraftForWrite` refuses a draft whose current state alone is over the budget (`HubDraftTooLargeError`) — nothing is written, and the couple reads "Your Event Hub is too large to save — remove a layer or an image."
- `lib/hub-draft-store.ts` — `writeHubDraft` writes only what `hubDraftForWrite` hands back. `saveHubDraftPatch` takes the form's way back: a failed save now redirects to the form's `return_to` with `draft_error=too_large|failed` instead of throwing. Every redirecting draft door passes it (widgets, backdrop, hero, the words door `draftEventsAndReturn`); Camera cues, which returns a result, returns the specific words.
- The Maker toolbar says it: `launch/page.tsx` → `HubDraftDock` → `HubDraftToolbar` shows `draft_error` as an alert beside Apply (and `/website/editor`'s forward into the Maker carries it). `hubDraftAction` returns the too-large sentence instead of the generic one.
- The ⋯ panel follows the answer (`hubDraftPanelStaysOpen`): it opens on an error, a key Apply held back, or Reset's note, and closes on a clean Apply · Undo · Restore, which now reads "Live now" beside Apply.
- Tests: `lib/the-draft-always-fits.test.ts` (new; each rule sabotaged once and seen red); `the-toolbar-never-hides-restore-undo-apply.test.ts` updated for the panel rule.

SPEC IMPACT: None — no schema change; the 200,000-byte CHECK is unchanged.
