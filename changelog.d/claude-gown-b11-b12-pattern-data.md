### Attire Maker B11 + B12 (pure parts only, nothing on screen yet): seam allowances, cutting list, 1:1 export

- **Seam allowances** (owner D29 and D30, 28 Nov: on by default, editable, centimetres):
  - every pattern piece gets a cut line at the set distance from each sewing edge, with mitred corners and very sharp points squared off;
  - no allowance on a fold, the zip allowance on a centre back cut as a pair, the hem allowance on a skirt or sleeve hem, the seam allowance everywhere else;
  - a net pattern has no cut line;
  - the shop's options can now carry her default allowances (0–10 cm; absent means 1.5 / 4 / 2 cm, on).
- **Cutting list:**
  - every piece with how many to cut, fold or pair, outer or lining, and its size including the allowance;
  - the metres use the prototype's own rule (150 cm cloth folded double, length plus 10 cm per width, at most two widths, plus 15 %), so they can sit beside the bride's quote. A gap over 10 % is flagged.
- **1:1 export** (owner D28: A4 and US Letter):
  - a map page with a 5 cm test square (50.0 mm in the file), the notes, "ESTIMATE: measure before cutting" when a number is estimated, and the grid of pages;
  - one tile per page, with corner marks and row and column names, butting together exactly. Blank pages are not printed;
  - pdf-lib loads only on export.
- No price, amount or money anywhere on a pattern.
- The screens (shop defaults, the order view's allowance row, net-pattern switch, cutting list and Export) come later, ported from the coordinator's prototype version.
