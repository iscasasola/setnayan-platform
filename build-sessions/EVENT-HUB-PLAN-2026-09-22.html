Event Hub, Pro, and the Controller

A build plan measured against the shipped code and the live database, not against what earlier documents claimed. Every line is marked SHIPS, BUILD, or YOUR CALL.

Part one

Event Hub — the free guest site

Twelve pages a guest can land on: /, /avatar, /find-my-table, /find-seat, /hub, /invite, /pabuya, /print, /recap, /seat, /venue, /welcome.

Build · highest priority

The coordinator’s announcement reaches one page in twelve

DayOfAnnouncement is mounted in exactly one file — site-body.tsx — and only [slug]/page.tsx renders that. A guest reading their seat, the floor plan, the gallery or the recap when “phones down, the ceremony is starting” goes out never sees it.

The fix is to lift it into the layout that wraps all twelve, so it follows the reader. Make it sticky while an announcement is live.

Measured: grep -rln "DayOfAnnouncement" apps/web/app returns the component and site-body.tsx. grep -rln "SiteBody" returns [slug]/page.tsx only.

Build

The dress code has no idea who is reading it

events.dress_code_config (migration 20260605030000) is one JSONB with five keys: title, description, palette, dos, donts. There is no role dimension anywhere, and the only role= in the dress-code editor is ARIA markup.

A ninang needs different words from a guest, and what she actually needs most is her call time — 3:00 PM, chapel vestry, called by name. Key it on RoleGroup, not on the individual role: lib/role-groups.ts already folds all 27 roles into nine groups, so the couple writes six rules instead of twenty-seven.

Shape: keep the shared palette; add byRole: Record<RoleGroup, {headline, note, palette?, callTime?}>.

Their logo on the hero — generated mark, or uploaded file?

A generated monogram already ships: resolveEventMonogramSvg reads events.monogram_text / _color / _style / _font_key / _frame_key, and AnimatedMonogramHero animates it. I put that on the prototype hero.

Build

Google Drive becomes a Pro prerequisite

Every manual upload is stored in the couple’s own Drive. That makes Drive not a feature but a precondition: Pro cannot do its job until one is connected, so the offer has to say so before payment, not after.

Drive photo-delivery is already connected in production, so the integration exists. What is new is the gate and the honest copy on the offer.

Worth deciding: what happens if a couple disconnects Drive after paying? Their uploads are in their own account, so the hub would be pointing at files it can no longer read.

Part three

The Controller — and why it is not taking care of the Hub

The controller lives at /dashboard/[eventId]/launch; /website redirects into it (merged 2026‑09‑02 on your ruling that the Papic pattern should apply here too). It is a status stage — standing, facts, next step, view-as, the Pro offer. It is good at telling the couple where they are.

What it is not good at is being the door to the Hub’s own settings.

Build

Picking stories — manual, or from an event you shared

What ships at /website/stories is the host choosing which vendor-created content appears on their celebration (your 2026‑08‑15 ruling). That is not what you asked for.

What you asked for is two sources: manual upload, and pull from another event the couple were both at. The second is the interesting one and it does not exist — it needs a query for events where both partners were guests, and a consent rule, because those photos belong to somebody else’s wedding.

Build

Schedule — manual, or adopt the coordinator’s

The couple builds a schedule at /dashboard/[eventId]/schedule, and the controller does link to it. A coordinator run-of-show exists separately (lib/schedule-ros.ts).

What is missing is the choice: one control that says “use what the coordinator set” and keeps it in sync, versus “I will write my own”. Needs a rule for what happens when the coordinator changes theirs after the couple adopted it.
