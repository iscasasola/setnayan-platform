### Gown maker A9: the bride's flow

- **One step at a time, with the stage on top.** The order is the owner's (27 Nov): Height, Weight, Body shape, Bust, Waist, Hips.
- **Then "Fine-tune her proportions?"** Yes adds these steps:
  - Bust position
  - Underbust
  - Shoulders
  - Arm length
  - Leg length
  - Elevation
  - What matters to her
- **Skip sits in the bottom row of each fine-tune step,** beside Next (v202). It puts back what she changed on the step, and that number is the mannequin's estimate again.
- **Each step opens on its own close-up of her (v204).** The close-up is measured on her body as she stands now: her bust point, her waist, her feet in these heels.
  - She turns so the part faces the view.
  - A measurement step holds its close-up for the whole step.
  - A design step holds a moment, then eases out to her whole figure.
  - A drag always wins.
- **A body type keeps her weight** (v210): she takes that shape at the same weight. A new weight keeps her shape, or keeps the girths she gave.
- **The design steps follow v210's order**, starting with:
  - Your colours
  - Silhouette
  - Fabric (the shop's fabrics only)
  - Colour
  - Sleeves
  - Cape and its details
  - Neckline, straps, back, length, train and veil

  Then the add-ons and the inside. Each list hides what the design rules out. A pick follows the pairing rules and says what else changed.
- **Undo** steps back through her last 40 changes.
- **Units** are cm and kg, or inches and lb (height in feet and inches).
- **The double-check note** is a small card on top of the stage. She can close it.
- **Lab:** `/dev/gown-maker-lab?view=designer` walks the whole flow on a sample seed, with no network.
