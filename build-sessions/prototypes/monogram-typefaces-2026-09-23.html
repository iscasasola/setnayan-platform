The typeface library

Every face here is OFL or Apache licensed on Google Fonts, so we can self-host the .ttf — which is the entry requirement, because the monogram engine reads glyph outlines and bakes them into the exported SVG. One library, shared by the monogram and your event page.

What is deliberately not here. The faces people picture when they say “wedding font” — Didot, Bodoni proper, Cochin, Copperplate, Snell Roundhand, Burgues Script, Adobe Caslon — are commercially licensed. They cannot be self-hosted or baked into an exported SVG without buying a licence per face, and a monogram export is a redistribution. The closest free equivalents are in the list and named below. Buying a small set of commercial faces is a real option, and an owner call — roughly US$30–250 per family for a web + embedding licence.
