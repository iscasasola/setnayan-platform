The six roles (your rulings, 19 Nov)
  • Shop Manager — sees everything, including branches.
  • Branch Manager — the same, for one branch.
  • Account Manager — their own clients on a branch: bookings, chat, payments, event day.
  • Marketing — shop page, cards, photos, promos, ads. No bookings, no money.
  • Event Crew — Hub and photos for the events they're given.
  • Helper — sees the schedule, changes nothing.

Decided 19 Nov Marketing covers the whole shop · ONE role per person · money changes need no second Shop Manager's yes.
