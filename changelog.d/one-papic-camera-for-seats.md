## 2026-11-22 · feat(papic): one camera — a seat opens the approved camera, full screen; the Event Hub keeps its opening seen

Owner, 22 Nov 2026, on his iPhone (host account, event `cale-ice`, Seat 150): *"when i took a photo, i cannot take another until it uploads"* · *"my issue is the first shot. took around 30 seconds"* · *"this is how the app looks like. and it is not nice"* · *"tag and (i) are taking up their own rows which consumes the space"* · *"we only have one camera interface and that should be it"* · *"a no scroll full screen camera page"* · *"when we return to the event hub. we don't want them to start at the reveal. we want them back to where they were last"*.

**What was wrong.** A seat camera — the host's own camera (sent there from the Hub's camera since commit `9853c4ed2`, earlier the same day) and every camera a host hands out — opened its own older screen, `papic-seat-capture.tsx`, not the approved camera (`prototypes/papic_camera_complete_2026-10-24_fable.html`, DECISION_LOG 2026-10-24 "ONE PAPIC CAMERA"). It drew "Tag who's in it" and the ⓘ on rows of their own, filed the roll under a "Your credits" label, floated "Add credits" over it, and ran the first photo's face pass in the foreground. Measured in production: the first photo of that session reached the server 1.8 s after its shutter (`papic_photos.created_at − captured_at`), so the 30-second wait was on the phone, not the network.

**What changed.**
- `/papic/seat/[token]` mounts the ONE camera (`PapicGuestCapture`) in a new **seat mode**, full screen and locked (no scroll). The old screen is deleted.
- New uploader lane `'seat'` (`lib/papic-upload/`): a seat's shot is saved on the phone first and sent in the background like every other Papic shot; a seat sends only its original (a clip: poster + raw) — the server makes the copies, as it always has.
- New route `app/api/papic/seat-capture` (JSON only): signs the one key a tier may be written to in the seat's own folder (`papic/event-<event>/seat-<seat>/…`, the folder `papicSeatCapturePolicy` already pins), and records through **`recordSeatCapture` unchanged** — the seat's whole fence (window, paid order, put-away, uploads switch, geo control, burst limiter, 10-second cap) and its meter (`papic_record_seat_capture`, credit + insert in one transaction). Nothing about price or who may shoot moved. New: a retry whose first answer was lost finds the row by the shot's own key and is never recorded or charged twice.
- Seat mode keeps the seat's own parts: tagging through `tagSeatCapture`, face vectors to `autoTagSeatCapture` (computed while the phone is quiet, off the shutter), the location stamp when `papic_geo_metadata` is on (it is, in production), the "running low" state (never the figure — PRIV-1), and seat words for a kept shot (window · paid order · put away · uploads off · reissued). The guest-only parts — photo terms, Kwento, Challenges, the face step, the public-sharing line, "Add from your phone" — are not drawn for a seat.
- ↩️ **The ✕ returns to where you were.** The Hub's camera link now carries the tab into the host's camera (`hostCameraHref(token, back)`), and the Event Hub records its own opening as seen once shown (`reveal-overlay.tsx`: `oncePerVisit` 'defer' now records too), so a return in the same visit never replays the reveal.

**Guards.** New `lib/papic-seat-lane.test.ts` (every `recordSeatCapture` refusal — read from its source — keeps the shot: never 'ok', never 'drop'; money refusals → credits; a seat's key sits in the seat folder) — sabotage-checked (403 → 200 turned it red). Twelve guards that read the old screen were re-pointed at the one camera / the seat route, none loosened: `the-camera-is-the-whole-screen` (now RENDERS the camera in seat mode: fixed stage, no guest rows), `every-camera-has-a-way-back`, `the-selfie-is-quick`, `the-uploads-switch-is-real` (the seat route is the recorder), `papic-exhaustion-truth`, `papic-pool-balance-is-the-couples`, `papic-face-mode-gate`, `capture-window-is-a-whole-day`, `putaway-stops-captures`, `papic-unit-is-credits`, `a-hosts-live-shot-costs-the-camera-price`, `a-save-never-resets-the-screen`; plus `a-queued-shot-records-only-where-it-was-taken` (seat route checks claimer + event before sign/record/attach), `papic-host-door`, `reveal-once-per-visit`.

**Not done here, named.** A seat clip no longer makes its small web copy on the phone after recording (the old screen's `persistSeatClipWebCopy` step): the raw stays the playable copy, so a seat clip's full-resolution original is not dropped by the retention sweep until a web copy exists. No personal credits count shows on a seat camera (it would show the couple's pot to a handed-out camera); a credit refusal turns the camera to its "credits ran out" state.
