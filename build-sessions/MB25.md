## The drawing is done and is in this folder

`build-sessions/assets/mb25/ceremony-church.svg` (sha256 `9c311f0f…`), preview beside it.
Generated 2026-09-05 by oversight on the owner's instruction, with **Recraft V4.1 `vector`** —
the same model family as the 75 attire figures (`source = 'higgsfield_generated'`) — from a
prompt that pinned a placeholder palette, so the recolourable regions arrived as two flat fills:

| region | placeholder fill | count of paths | what it covers |
|---|---|---|---|
| florals | `#D98BA6` (`rgb(217,139,166)`) | 64 | the altar arch, the pew-end clusters |
| fabric | `#E8D9B5` (`rgb(232,217,181)`) | 35 | aisle runner, pew ribbons, candle bases |

Everything else is neutral: pews `#8A6A4E`, walls `#F4F1EA`, floor `#D6D1C7`, window glass
white. 326 paths, **no gradients, no rasters**. Viewbox `0 0 2048 1331` (3:2). Four variants were
generated; the other three were rejected because the flowers came out in the fabric colour (two)
or the pews came out red (one) — a colour the couple never chose is exactly the disease this
section just had.

**Measured colour distances (CIELAB ΔE, every fill in the file):**

- nearest non-placeholder fill to the **florals** slot: **38.2** — the slot is isolated.
- nearest non-placeholder fill to the **fabric** slot: **14.4** (the floor grey `#D6D1C7`); walls
  are at 17.9. **Fabric tolerance must be ≤ 10.** MB23's seeded `15` would have painted the floor.

A simulated recolour (florals → burgundy, fabric → gold, by fill swap) renders cleanly: only the
arch, clusters, runner, ribbons and candle bases change. Oversight looked at it.

## Slots, in the card's own terms

`page.tsx` builds the Ceremony card with `paletteColors: palette.ceremony` and the board maps
**`slot_id` N → the N-th ceremony colour** (`out[r.slotId] = palette[i % palette.length]` in
`moodboard-board.tsx`). So: `slot_id 1` = florals (the couple's first ceremony colour),
`slot_id 2` = fabric (their second). A couple with one ceremony colour gets both regions in it,
which is what `i % palette.length` already does.
