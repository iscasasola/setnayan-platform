## 2026-08-05 · feat(vendor): vendors get invited into partnerships instead of having to find them

Partnerships were finished on both sides, reachable, and used by nobody —
because nothing ever suggested them. A vendor had to already know the feature
existed and go looking for it.

It now appears in the "Grow your business" list on a vendor's performance page,
alongside the other honest suggestions there: reply faster, add photos, ask for
reviews. It says the true thing — when a coordinator or venue you work with
vouches for you, you show up in their couples' results with their name on it.

It follows the same rule as every other card on that list: **a vendor who has
already done it never sees it.** Three live partnerships and the suggestion
stops. Two and it drops down the list. It also states the real count rather than
inventing a number about what partnerships are worth, because we don't have one.

If the count can't be read, the card is hidden rather than shown — a failed
count comes back as zero, which looks exactly like "you have none", and nagging
a vendor who already has ten is worse than staying quiet.
