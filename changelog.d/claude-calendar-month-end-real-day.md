## 2026-11-10 · fix(calendar): the supplier month reads to its real last day, and a refused month says so (G7)

Production had logged `vendor_calendar_day_states` 400 `date out of range: "2026-11-31"` on /vendor-dashboard/customers since 4 Sep. Every month read asked `${month}-01` → `${month}-31`, and `fetchVendorDayStates` turned the refusal into `[]`. So in every 30-day month and every February, held and approve-first days were drawn as free. Three call sites (`customers/page.tsx` month, and both branches of `customers/actions.ts` `fetchCustomerCalendarMonth`) now go through `fetchVendorMonthDayStates` / `monthDayRange` in `lib/vendor-schedule.ts`, which get the last day from the existing `lastDayOf` (`lib/event-date-floor.ts`). `fetchVendorDayStates` now logs a refusal and returns null, not `[]`. The Clients month shows "This month's held days couldn't load…" (first paint and a month paged to), the one-day sheet shows the same for its hold, and the picked client's Schedule month falls back to its existing "couldn't load" line. Guard `lib/vendor-month-reads-its-real-last-day.test.ts` covers Feb 28/29, Apr 30, Nov 30 and Dec 31, the range actually asked, null on refusal, the on-screen lines, and any `${…}-31` month end in source. Each of its four sabotages was confirmed to fail before the fix was trusted.
