## How this was counted

- **Scope:** `apps/web/app`, `apps/web/components`, `apps/web/lib`. Tests, `app/dev/**` and `*.lab.tsx` are exempt (guardrail D17).
- **Areas:** *supplier* = `app/vendor-dashboard`, `app/v/[slug]`, `app/for-suppliers` and `app/(shell)/for-suppliers`, `app/open-shop`, `app/vendor`, `app/vendor-invite`.
  *Couple and guest* = `app/dashboard`, the guest Event Hub `app/[slug]`, `app/(shell)` public pages, onboarding, Papic, Panood, pay, login and the rest of `app/`.
  *Admin* = `app/admin`. *Shared* = `app/_components`, `components`, `lib`, `app/globals.css`: the "fix once" list.
- **Greps run** (from `apps/web`, then every hit opened to classify):
  `gradient` · `shadow-` and CSS `box-shadow` · tinted `bg-*-50/100/200` or `bg-<accent>/N` on a centred, square, rounded box whose child is an icon ·
  `rounded-[` · `Welcome back` / `Good morning` / `% from last` / `vs last` / `vs prev` / `👋` · grids of 3–4 equal numeric cards
  (`grid-cols-3|4`, `KpiStatCard`, `StatCard`, `StatTile`, `PartTiles`) · `.tsx` files that format a number (`formatPhp`, `formatCount`,
  `toLocaleString`, `Intl.NumberFormat`) and never set `tabular-nums` (also checked the CSS module each file imports).
- **Confidence.** ✔ = the line was opened and read. ◐ = matched by the scanner and its context (static, not inside a sheet, menu,
  toast or fixed bar), not read line by line. ◐ rows are real candidates; the builder confirms each before changing it.
  Floating surfaces keep their shadow.
- **Not counted (allowed by the standard or product data), so not padded in:**
  the readable band under text on a photo (B6) · the dark-mode top-block glow `--color-band-glow` (Part I exception) · the skeleton Sheen ·
  scroll-edge fades and masks · gradients with hard stops that paint a flat colour (meter fills, split dots, the `linear-gradient(tint, tint)` trick) ·
  hatching that means "not available" · product art and couple-picked themes: invite themes (`app/[slug]/invite/_components/themes`), the
  approved badge and award art (`app/_components/award`), the gift box, monogram and wax-stamp preview stages, cover backgrounds a couple picks
  (`lib/cover-scene.ts`, stage panel), print designs (`lib/supplier-prints.ts`), ambient effects, colour wheels in pickers, the Event Hub's
  `--hub-radius` theme radii, phone and device mock-ups.
- **No purple-to-blue gradient exists anywhere** (0 hits for `from-purple|violet|indigo|fuchsia`).
