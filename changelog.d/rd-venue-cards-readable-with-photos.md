## 2026-09-30 · fix(event-hub): the venue cards are readable, and each one shows its supplier's photo (or the couple's own)

Owner, on `cale-ice`'s Venue scene: *"I cannot see the venues properly."* · *"The venue will have their Photos … derived from the supplier's account"* · *"same as the address"* · *"but if there is none, then we can upload it manually"* · *"so click on it. use supplier details. or input your data"*.

- **Readable.** Measured live at 390 px: the venue name painted rgb(243 231 220) on its card's rgb(240 237 232), which is 1.04 : 1. The theme was the dark `velvet`, whose plate ink is LIGHT, and the couple's mood-board palette, spread inline over it, repainted the card LIGHT without naming a plate ink. `pinPlateInk` (`app/[slug]/_lib/pro-site-vars.ts`) now runs last in `guestLookFrom` and keeps `--color-ink-on-plate` only when it reads on the card that actually paints. The fix covers every `.pahina-plate` and the reply card, not just the venues. The directions chips now stand on the card instead of the page ground (1.1 : 1 before). The address, the "Ceremony"/"Reception" label and "Get directions" moved to 80 % ink. After: name 17.3 : 1 · address and labels 10.5 : 1 · buttons 8.8 : 1.
- **No empty band.** The pale gradient where a picture should be is gone. With no photo, the card is text only.
- **The supplier's photo.** A booked platform supplier's public shop photos (`vendor_profiles.portfolio_r2_keys`, the shop page's own list and gate) are shown. The first one is the default. The couple picks another, or none, in Details › Venues. If there is no supplier photo, or the couple wants their own, they upload one (shipped `FileUpload`, compressed in the browser, into the Maker's scene-media folder). The photo shows before a reply because it reveals no more than the venue name. The address, pin and directions still wait for the reply.
- **Use the supplier's details, or enter your own.** Tapping a venue card on a stage opens Details › Venues in place. Each card offers one dropdown: "Use the supplier's details" or "Enter your own". Switching back keeps what was typed.
- Choices are stored in the Venue scene's own `config_json.venue`. There is no new table, column, migration or server action: they save through `saveAllStdContent`, which the panel already uses.
- Guards: `lib/the-venue-cards-are-readable.test.ts` measures the blended pixel of every venue word on all 10 themes and 8 colour sources. `lib/the-venue-card-shows-the-suppliers-photo.test.ts` covers the photo, the source choice, the reply gate and the stage tap.

SPEC IMPACT: None: the decision row "A VENUE'S PHOTO COMES FROM ITS SUPPLIER — AND THE COUPLE CAN UPLOAD ONE IF THERE IS NONE" (DECISION_LOG 2026-09-30) is already in the corpus, and this implements it.
