### Gown maker: the cloth's surface: weave, lace and cut-outs (step A6c)

- The fabric's own weave at thread scale (threads, grain, net, pile), so satin, crepe, tulle or linen read true up close; it only shades, never changes the colour.
- Lace appliqué: Floral, Alençon, Chantilly, Guipure, Leaf and a Scallop border, placed at real size as a cascade, on the bodice, along the neckline edge, along the real hem (over a train), scattered or all over; the motif count for the designer matches prototype v195 exactly. A border lace goes on the hem.
- Cut-outs: side waist, keyhole, hip and distressed slashes (the under-bust and back cut-outs stay forbidden), with clean, bound, frayed, scalloped or beaded edges, open or filled with illusion mesh; a cropped top leaves a band of skin. The rule hand-cut holes will follow (A7) is in place.
- The cloth's surface shader is v195's own, extracted verbatim and checked on this three.js.
- Dev lab only (`/dev/gown-maker-lab?view=dress`, the Lace and Cut-out pickers); nothing a person sees yet.
