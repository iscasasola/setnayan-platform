## 2026-09-16 · fix(privacy): a guest who asked to be blurred is blurred on the couple's PUBLIC page too

**The gap was named in this repo on 2026-09-09 and left open on purpose.** `lib/every-guest-read-asks-the-blur-gate.test.ts` carried it verbatim: the public recap's own gate (`app/[slug]/_components/editorial/consent-veto.ts` → `publicKeyForCapture`) implements only the **withdrawn-consent** half of owner ruling 1 in TypeScript and has **no FaceBlock arm at all**, while `papic_capture_needs_blur` treats FaceBlock as **event-wide**. So on an event with a FaceBlock guest the venue wall and the shared pool blurred every frame and **the couple's public event page did not** — the one surface a stranger can reach.

Owner ruling 1 of 2026-08-17: *"Public = everyone except the couple — blur on the venue wall, the public event page, and the shared pool other guests browse. The couple's own album stays unblurred."* Two of the three were built. This is the third.

🔑 **ONE GATE, AND THE ARM IS NOT WRITTEN IN TYPESCRIPT.** Writing `faceblock_enabled` into `consent-veto.ts` is exactly what `lib/papic-guest-blur-gate.ts` refuses to do and what its own test forbids — and re-implementing the withdrawal half in TypeScript is *how this hole was dug*. So migration `20271229892059` lifts the event-wide arm out of `papic_capture_needs_blur` into `papic_event_blurs_every_capture` and **redefines `papic_capture_needs_blur` in terms of it**. The FaceBlock clause now exists in exactly one place in the system; the wall and the pool reach it through `papic_capture_needs_blur`, and the recap asks it directly because it holds an event, not a list of captures. They cannot disagree — there is nothing to disagree with. `papic_capture_needs_blur` computes the same boolean as before, character for character; the nine assertions in `withdrawal-blurs-and-keeps.db.test.ts` and the pool's tests pass untouched.

🔑 **THE ANSWER GOES IN `ids`, NOT IN A FLAG BESIDE IT.** The obvious shape — an `eventWideBlur` boolean on `ConsentVeto` — would have been a **new hole in six places**: the recap's hero pick, three reads in `_components/story/spine-data.ts`, `lib/story-arrangement-store.ts` and `lib/story-cover.ts` all inspect `veto.ids` **directly** instead of calling `publicKeyForCapture`, and `spine-data.ts` short-circuits on `veto.ids.size === 0`. A FaceBlock event with nobody withdrawn has an empty tag set, so every one of those six would have sailed straight past a flag. Folding FaceBlock into `ids` fixes all six with **no edit outside this module** and leaves `publicKeyForCapture` itself unchanged.

⚠ **A MEASURED HOLE CLOSED ON THE WAY, AND IT WIDENS THE DIFF ON PURPOSE.** The gate accepted `safe_display_r2_key ?? wall_safe_r2_key` as a blurred stand-in. **`wall_safe_r2_key` is not proof of a blur:** `wall_ingest` stamps it with `COALESCE(wall_safe_r2_key, r2_object_key)`, so on a capture that needed no blur at ingest it holds **the unblurred original**. Measured in production 2026-09-16: of 25 `papic_photos`, exactly **1** carries a `wall_safe_r2_key` and it is **identical to its `r2_object_key`**. Event-wide blur would have made that reachable on every frame. A stand-in is now trusted only with `faceblock_baked_at` set and `photo_type <> 'clip'` — the same demand `wall_visible_photos`, `guest_pool_gallery` and `papic-guest-blur-gate.ts` have all made since 2026-08-24. This tightens the withdrawal arm too, in the withholding direction only.

⚖ **THIS CHANGES A PAGE THAT IS ALREADY PUBLIC, and that is the point rather than a side effect.** Once a guest turns FaceBlock on, every Papic frame on that couple's published recap must carry a baked blur or be **withheld** — frames people have already seen stop being served. Production carries **0 baked captures**, so on today's data the outcome would be withheld, not blurred. That is the posture the wall and the pool have had since 2026-08-24 and what ruling 1 asks for, but it is a change to something already published and is named here rather than discovered later.

🔎 **Inert today, which is why now is the moment.** Measured in production 2026-09-16: **118 live guests · 0 `faceblock_enabled` · 0 `face_recognition_excluded` · 0 withdrawn `photo_consent` · 25 papic captures · 0 baked.** Nobody is harmed today; the first FaceBlock guest will be a real person who asked not to be shown.

⛔ **Named, not widened:** arm 2 (withdrawn consent) still reads `source_table = 'papic_photos'` only, so a *guest capture* tagging a withdrawn guest is not vetoed by it — arm 1 covers both tables, arm 2 never has. And arm 2 keeps its `deleted_at IS NULL` filter, which the SQL predicate does **not** have: soft-deleting an opted-out guest lifts the recap's veto but not the wall's. That divergence is an **open owner question** already flagged in `app/dashboard/[eventId]/guests/[guestId]/actions.ts` (*"Whether that is the right rule is a question for the owner and is NOT changed here"*) and is not decided here either. Both are written into the module and into the test that measures the divergence.

SPEC IMPACT: `DECISION_LOG.md` — ruling 1 of 2026-08-17 is now complete across all three public surfaces (venue wall, shared pool, public event page). The 2026-09-09 row recording the public-recap FaceBlock gap as open is closed by this change; the NAMED-NOT-FIXED note in `lib/every-guest-read-asks-the-blur-gate.test.ts` is amended in place rather than deleted.
