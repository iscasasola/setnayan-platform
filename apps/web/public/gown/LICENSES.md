# Gown maker assets

## female.glb

- **What:** the faceless mannequin the gown maker dresses (one skinned body, 10,418 vertices, 105 bones).
- **Source:** base mesh by Innerscene Corp (innerscene.com), released under **CC0 1.0** (public domain dedication).
  Stylised as a faceless mannequin and decimated for Setnayan. The same credit is in the file's own glTF `asset.copyright`.
- **Taken from:** the approved prototype, corpus `prototypes/gown_maker_2026-11-27/mannequin/female.json` (the GLB was
  stored there as base64; it is unchanged here, byte for byte).
- **Women only:** the prototype's men's body (`male.json`) is not part of the platform.

## shoe-pump.json

- **What:** the bride's wedding shoe, a pointed-toe stiletto pump (heel, insole, pump: 1,149 vertices), with three
  landmarks (heel `H`, ball `B`, toes `T`) and the stiletto post the fitting drops straight to the floor.
- **Source:** 3D model from **cadnav.com**, supplied by the owner. Their terms allow use inside a project **with credit**,
  not resale of the model on its own (corpus `prototypes/gown_maker_2026-11-27/PROGRESS.md`, "Shoes"). The maker must
  show the credit line ("Shoe model: cadnav.com") wherever the prototype did (its Settings credits); A9 carries it.
- **Taken from:** the approved prototype v193 (`SHOE_MODEL`), unchanged.
