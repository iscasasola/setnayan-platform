## 2026-08-05 · feat: the couple can turn face tagging off for their own event

Face tagging was optional at every level except the one that matters most.

A **guest** chooses: the selfie step is skippable, the RSVP goes through without
it, and nothing is stored unless they tick consent and confirm they're 18 or
over. That's been true all along.

An **admin** chooses, per event.

The **couple** — whose wedding it is, and whose guests these are — had no say at
all. Now they do, on their Papic page.

It can only ever say no. A couple can switch face tagging off for their whole
event; they can't switch it on where it wasn't offered, and they can't override
the lock on christenings and debuts. Turning it back on only restores what was
already set — it never enables anything by itself.

The card doesn't appear at all when there's nothing to decline. A control that
can't actually change anything is a promise the product can't keep.
