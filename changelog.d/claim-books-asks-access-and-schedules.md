## 2026-10-20 · feat(claim): a claimed supplier arrives booked if paid, asks for access on its own, and shows on the schedule as Booked or Shortlisted

Owner ruling 2026-10-20 (DECISION_LOG "A CLAIMED SUPPLIER ARRIVES BOOKED IF PAID, ASKS FOR ACCESS ON ITS OWN…").

- **Booked if paid — derived, not backfilled.** `lib/booked-by-payment.ts` is the one resolver: a supplier the couple added by hand (no shop, or still carrying the couple's contact card) with ≥1 payment the supplier has not refused reads as booked. Applied at the load of every couple read that draws a supplier's standing — `fetchEventVendors` (Suppliers page), `fetchBudgetSnapshot` + the money resolver's I/O wrapper (Budget), `EventDashboard` (Home), `readScheduleSuppliers` (schedule). No stored status is rewritten.
- **The claim asks; the couple's Approve grants.** `applyClaimAutoLink` no longer bonds the couple's record to the shop (`marketplace_vendor_id` is what every supplier-side read keys on — the bond IS the access). It raises ONE `event_access_requests` row of the new kind `claim` (`claim_ask_for_access`) and an in-app `vendor_joined` notice (no email). Approve (`answerClaimAccess` → `answer_claim_access_request`) runs in one transaction: booked-if-paid catch-up of the stored status, the merge-aware bond, the shipped sibling pass, and a host-level seat (`COORDINATOR_AREAS`) for a coordinator. Decline writes only the answer.
- **The approval card** (`ClaimAccessCard`: Approve · Decline) on Event Details › Event access ("Waiting for you") and Access requests; Home's existing access doorway counts it.
- **Schedule.** Items name their supplier by shop name with "Booked" / "Shortlisted" (item sheet chips, the tag picker, the View-as lens, and a new line on the rail row). `claim_link_record_to_shop` now re-points `event_schedule_blocks.responsible_vendor_ids` on a merge (a uuid[] the FK loop never saw).
