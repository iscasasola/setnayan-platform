Live Studio, end to end

Everything a wedding needs, in the order it needs it — what works today, what a human still does by hand, and what is genuinely missing.

Live Studio works today. A couple can buy it, connect phones, switch cameras, broadcast, and keep the film in their story afterwards. Everything below is about the seams — the places where a human fills a gap the software should.

The path a wedding takes

Buy Live Studio — ₱2,500, one unlockworks

One unlock per event. Unlimited streams, no day limit. Four warnings show before payment: our 24-hour by-hand approval SLA (hence the advice to buy at least 2 days ahead), YouTube's 24-hour activation, the laptop, and the music risk. “Unlimited video links” is promised by the catalog description but not yet delivered — see step 12. A tick box gates checkout until they confirm their channel is ready — asked once, ever.

Payment reconciledby hand · 24h

Apply-then-pay. A human checks every payment. This is why the buy screen says "buy at least 2 days before" — an unlock bought the night before may not clear in time.

The channel question — theirs, or yourshalf in flight

Default (their own YouTube): works. They create the broadcast, paste the watch link, no Google authorisation ever touches Setnayan. Hosted (Setnayan's channel): the SKU is currently deactivated — LS8 is reactivating it at ₱3,000/day.

YouTube activation — 24 hourscouple, once ever

Their channel must be live-enabled. First time takes ~24 hours and nothing can shorten it. Disclosed at purchase, with the check page named and the 50-subscriber myth pre-empted (that rule is phone-app only).

Cameras joinworks

Phones scan the event QR. No install, no per-camera fee, up to 12. WebRTC over the internet — a camera can be on mobile data or at another venue, which every competitor's LAN-based product cannot do.

The laptop + OBSthe real gap

A browser cannot push a livestream. Someone must run OBS on a Windows or Mac laptop, window-capturing the program output. Disclosed before purchase — but it is the friction the nine-session encoder plan exists to remove.

Audioconfigured in OBS

The camera phones' audio rides the same WebRTC transport as their video, but OBS window-captures the program output's picture — sound is a separate OBS source the operator must set up. Get it wrong and the vows are silent while everything looks fine. This is one of the strongest reasons for the encoder plan: taking audio off the MediaStream as a track removes the OS routing step entirely.

Go liveworks

A pool channel is claimed, the YouTube broadcast is created and bound, and the watch URL is written back. Or the couple presses "We're on air" if they started the broadcast themselves.

Directingworks

Cut between cameras, split-screen at any ratio, monogram and lower third. The free tier gets one camera and POWERED BY SETNAYAN; ₱2,500 unlocks the rest. The paywall lives in the program output, so it cannot be routed around by using a different channel.

If the encoder diesvisible since 3 Sept

YouTube knows within ~10 seconds and now so does the controller. A stale poll reads as no_data, never "still fine", and a failed read says "cannot tell" rather than "receiving".

If the music is copyrighteddisclosed, not prevented

YouTube scans live and can cut the stream mid-ceremony — even for music the couple licensed, unless their channel is on the rights-holder allowlist. Warned before purchase, with the fix named (live musicians, or royalty-free).

Guests watch on the Event Hubworks

Embedded in their colours. Unlisted only when Setnayan creates the broadcast (pool channel or one-tap go-live) — on the default paste-a-link path the couple sets their own privacy, and a public broadcast is in YouTube search. Unlimited viewers at ₱0 — YouTube's CDN is doing something genuinely expensive for free.

Afterwards — the film staysPR failing CI

The replay appears on their story page and survives the broadcast ending (fixed 2 Sept — before that it could never render at all). Attaching other films — same-day edit, prenup, the videographer's cut — is PR #5140, open and FAILING its required check (an RLS policy scoped member-wide instead of couple-only, caught by couple-host-policy-scope.db.test.ts). It does not merge on its own.

The channel comes backautomatic after 24h

A checkout older than 24 hours whose streams are idle is reclaimed the next time the pool is empty. Before that window, an admin clicks Release. Deliberate: auto-release would strand a host who ends after the ceremony and restarts for the reception.

Three cases the path above does not cover

The free tier's route to air

One-tap go-live is gated on owning Live Studio. A free host's only route is their own YouTube plus OBS, and their program output is pinned to a single camera — cutting to another moves their monitor and deliberately not the air. So “free” means one camera, their own channel, their own encoder, with POWERED BY SETNAYAN burned in.

A multi-day celebration

The ₱2,500 unlock genuinely has no day limit. But two things are still per-day: the hosted channel is priced per day, and the pool reclaims a checkout older than 24 hours whose streams are idle — so on a two-day fiesta, night one's channel can be handed to another event before day two, if the pool is contended. With two claimable channels that is unlikely; it is not impossible, and nothing warns about it.

What guests see when it goes wrong

The controller is now honest about a dead encoder. The guest page is not covered: if the couple never pastes their watch link, guests simply see no stream and no explanation. That asymmetry — operator warned, guest silent — is the one place the day's work did not reach.

Owner actions — nothing engineering can do for you

Tick Verified on ICASA MODIFIED

Only after youtube.com/features shows Live streaming: Enabled for that specific channel. The tick is the only thing gating a channel into the pool, and nothing verifies it.

Reconnect the Setnayan channel

granted_at is still 2026-08-31. Whether that token carries a 7-day fuse depends on a publishing status nobody recorded. Two minutes closes the question.

File for a YouTube quota increase

Long before you need it — grants take time, and the ceiling bites on a busy Saturday.

Decide: Google Workspace

An Internal audience removes verification and the user cap permanently. Only possible with a Workspace or Cloud Identity org. Worth doing when you get Workspace for email anyway.

Decide: Windows code signing

A purchase and a few days. Without it, every Windows couple sees "Windows protected your PC" — the same friction as Google's unverified screen, in their wedding week.

What "fully functional" means

A couple buys Live Studio, opens the Setnayan app on a laptop, hands phones to friends, and presses Go live. Nothing else is installed, no stream key is pasted, no third-party software is configured. The broadcast reaches their guests on their own page, the controller tells them the truth about whether it is working, and afterwards the film sits in their story beside the photos. (Not guaranteed permanent: the buy screen's own music warning says a saved recording can still be claimed or muted by Content ID after the day.)

Every part of that sentence is true today except "opens the Setnayan app". That is the encoder, and it is the last structural gap. Everything else on this page is a click, a purchase decision, or a PR — though PR #5140 is currently failing its required check, not merely waiting.
