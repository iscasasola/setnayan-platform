### Gown maker v203: puff, bishop and Juliet sleeves, the ruffle band, cape sleeves and the over-robe

- **Five new sleeves:**
  - Puff: short and full, gathered at the shoulder and into a band above the elbow.
  - Bishop: long and full, gathered into a fitted cuff at the wrist.
  - Juliet: puffed at the top, then fitted along the forearm.
  - Ruffle band: a gathered ruffle round each upper arm, joined across the back. It sits on an off-shoulder or sweetheart neckline.
  - Cape sleeves: a band round each upper arm, with a sheer panel falling freely behind the arm, to the elbow, the fingertips, the floor or a train.
- **The over-robe:** a full-length open robe worn over the gown like a coat, to the floor or a train. It has its own sleeves (long, or puff gathered at the elbow), laid over her arm and any gown sleeve.
- **Clothes follow the body.** Where her arm lies against her body or the skirt, the cloth is pressed flat between them, never through it. A set-in sleeve starts where it leaves her body. Every new piece measures 0 mm inside her skin, as the prototype does.
- **Detachable sleeves:**
  - On a strapless bodice, the new sleeves sit on their own band round the upper arm.
  - Off the shoulder, she picks: "Sewn to the bodice" or "Detachable".
- **The pairings, with one line each:**
  - Cape sleeves and a cape take each other's place.
  - The over-robe goes with no flutter or butterfly sleeves.
  - A halter or one-shoulder strap and the new sleeves can't be worn together (both need both shoulders).
  - The ruffle band moves the neckline to off-shoulder.
  - On a Filipiniana, a new sleeve takes the butterfly sleeves' place.
  - The server refuses each pairing these rules rule out.
- **Words:**
  - The cape details carry the piece's own name ("Cape sleeves made of", "Over-robe length").
  - The sleeve and over-robe lines read as the prototype's summary does.
  - The quantity contract gains detachable sleeve bands and the over-robe.
- **Checked against prototype v203** (14 designs, 23 rules):
  - Every vertex of every sleeve, the ruffle across the back and the over-robe's sleeves.
  - The cape panels.
  - The sleeve metres.
  - The rules and the hidden choices, word for word.
