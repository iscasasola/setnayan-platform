### Gown maker: the v201/v202 strap fixes (prototype v202)

- A plunge to the waist and butterfly sleeves no longer sit on one gown. Choosing a plunge takes the butterfly sleeves off; choosing butterfly sleeves, or a Filipiniana, turns the plunge into a bateau neckline. Each change says why, word for word as prototype v202 does.
- The straps are now checked against v202 vertex for vertex. v202 writes a beaded strap's beads after its own rows, the same order this port already used.
- The illusion tulle line in the quote follows v202: only an illusion bodice, an illusion back or a plunge filled with mesh buys tulle. A scalloped top edge alone adds none.
- Not ported yet: v202's Skip in the bottom row. It waits for the measurement flow.
