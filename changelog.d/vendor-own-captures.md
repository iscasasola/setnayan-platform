## 2026-08-05 · feat(vendor): a vendor can finally see the photos they took

A booked vendor can shoot photos and clips on the day. Everything they shoot
goes into the couple's gallery — and until now the vendor could never look at
any of it again. They pressed the shutter and the pictures vanished into someone
else's album. No confirmation it worked, no way to check a shot landed.

Their own captures now appear directly under the camera, where the doubt is: on
a dark reception floor the question is "did that upload?", and the answer should
be on the same screen.

They see their own work and nothing else. A photo the couple takes down leaves
the vendor's view as well — it is the couple's event and the couple's call. And
a photo that has not yet been through the automatic content check is shown to
nobody, including the person who took it, so a just-taken shot appears a moment
later rather than immediately. The screen says so, because otherwise "my photo
is missing" is a support ticket.

If the list can't load, it says the photos are safe and only the view failed —
an empty grid would otherwise read as "your work is gone".

SPEC IMPACT: None — the permission for this already shipped; only the screen was
missing.
