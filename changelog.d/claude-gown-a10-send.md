### Gown maker A10: the send

- **What the design needs, measured on the gown itself** (prototype v206 `quoteLines`, the quantity half). After each full build, the stage measures:
  - the panels laid flat, and the cape, overlay and sleeves;
  - the bodice and the back opening, and the bones;
  - the round of the hem;
  - each trim's edge;
  - the lace motifs and the beads.

  These become the quantities the shop's quote reads: metres and counts, never a price. They are held to the prototype's own quote lines for five gowns.
- **Her hand work is counted only once.** Hand-placed beads and lace travel beside the design, and the shop's quote adds them itself, so the quantities leave them out.
- **The send screen**, reached as "Next: Review and send" after the last design step when she was invited from a chat:
  - the design's picture;
  - the design in words;
  - her main numbers in her units;
  - "Your measurements go to [shop] only.";
  - one Send.

  A refusal reads as its plain reason, with Try again.
- **What she sends** is the design, her body, the gown measurements the design needs (the mannequin's estimates, to the half cm), the quantities, and her hand work beside the design. Each passes the shop's own checks.
- **`GownStill`**, the design's picture: the 2D drawing first, in the gown's own colour. Once it is on screen, one still 3D frame takes its place, and then the stage stops drawing.
- **Lab:** `/dev/gown-maker-lab?view=send` (a sample shop).

SPEC IMPACT: None (plan A10; prototype v206 quote quantities, v210 tile shapes; the privacy line is the plan's approved Q6 wording).
