### Attire Maker B13: scan a swatch book

- Shop › Attire Maker › Fabrics now has a "Scan a swatch book" button.
- The designer adds photos of swatch-book pages. Every swatch on each page is found on her phone, and each one gets a numbered box she can tap, drag, resize, split, delete, or add when one was missed.
- Each swatch is shown straightened and colour-corrected against the card paper, with its true size from the card's width. It gets a bridal colour name, or her own name when she already stocks a near-identical colour. Notes point out a pattern bigger than the swatch, or a shadow.
- Swatches that share a code family form one style, and a style's swatches are its colours. For each style she picks the kind of cloth and, if she wants, her own price per metre.
- Save creates one fabric per style through the existing fabric save. A style or photo already saved is never saved twice.
- Nothing leaves the phone until Save. Brides never see this screen.
- Not ported: the prototype's AI label reader (owner, Q10). Names and codes are typed by hand.
- Lab: `/dev/gown-shop-lab?view=scan`.
