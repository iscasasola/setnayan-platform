## 2026-11-06 · feat(signup): one email at sign-up — Supabase's "Confirm signup" is the welcome; the app's own welcome stops

Until now a new account got two emails: Supabase Auth's "Confirm signup" and the app's own plain-text Resend "Welcome to Setnayan · Your vendor|couple account is ready. Sign in here". The owner chose one. The confirm email becomes a warm welcome for each door (template designed in the spec corpus), and the app stops sending its own.

- **Removed** the `sendEmail({ subject: 'Welcome to Setnayan', … })` slot from the `Promise.allSettled` in `app/signup/actions.ts` (`signUp`) and in `lib/open-shop-account.server.ts` (`createVendorAccountForShop`), along with their `emailResult` logging. The confirm flip and the profile write still run side by side, and `autoConfirmed` / `confirmed` still come from the confirm flip alone. `welcomeEmailKind` (`lib/signup-landing.ts`) no longer had a caller, so it is gone. The guest-from-an-invitation case it covered is now the general rule.
- **Kept:** the anonymous-convert path's "Your Setnayan plan is secured" email in `app/signup/actions.ts`. That path calls `updateUserById`, not `auth.signUp`, so no Supabase email goes out and this is the only email that person gets.
- **Added** `signup_door` to `auth.signUp`'s `options.data` beside `account_type`: `'couple'` from `/signup`, `'supplier'` from `/signup?as=vendor` and from the shop wizard. It is user metadata (JSON), so there is no migration. **No `'coordinator'` yet:** no sign-up door on `main` knows it is the coordinator door, because the services choice is written after sign-up. The template's coordinator branch therefore does not fire yet, and a coordinator gets the supplier welcome, which is still true for them.
- **Added** `supabase/templates/confirm-signup.html` for the record: a byte-for-byte copy of the specs repo's `prototypes/welcome_emails_2026-11-07/confirm-signup.template.html` under a header. The header says the owner pastes it into Supabase › Auth › Email Templates › Confirm signup (subject "Confirm your email — welcome to Setnayan") and that no config wires it.
- **Guards:** `lib/guest-one-path.test.ts` test 5 now says no `/signup` path mails a welcome; it no longer checks that the welcome is gated on the event question. `lib/the-journey-words-say-what-happens.test.ts` test 3 (the welcome email's link) is replaced by two tests. One checks that neither door calls `sendEmail` with a "Welcome to Setnayan" subject, and every `sendEmail` subject there must be a literal so the check can read it. The other checks that each door passes a `signup_door` the template reads.

⚠ **Merge order:** the owner pastes the template into Supabase FIRST. Merged before that, a new account gets only Supabase's default "Confirm your signup" email.
