### Gown maker A8: poses and motion on the stage

- **Her poses:** Standing, Arms down, Hand on hip, Step, Knee pop, Mid-stride and Kneel.
  - A change of pose blends over 0.45 s.
  - Her arms ease out a little at a time until her hands clear her body and the skirt.
  - Hand on hip reaches her left hand to the side of the skirt, just under the waist.
  - In heels she stands on her toes.
  - When she steps, kneels or sits, the skirt keeps the length and the hips it was cut for standing. A thigh or knee pushes the cloth out; it never re-cuts it.
  - A floor-length cape still hides while she kneels.
- **Movement:** an aisle walk with the bouquet held still, an everyday walk, a runway walk and the bridal photo jump.
  - The body and gown are rebuilt about 24 times a second while she moves.
  - The painted lace and cut-outs ride on the cloth, and the beads follow it.
  - The floor eases under her and the view stays put.
- **Twirls:** a classic twirl, a slow reveal, a double twirl and a sway.
  - Each starts slow, builds speed and stops smoothly.
  - With reduced motion she just turns round.
- **Turning:** the auto-turn (slow or fast) goes round and round, or does a showroom turn that rests at the front, each side and the back, or sways.
  - The skirt follows her turn, and also a drag round her.
- **The stage's own buttons:**
  - **Pose:** a tap steps to the next pose, or stops a walk. Hold it for every pose and movement.
  - **Twirl:** a tap twirls. Hold it for the style.
  - **Turn:** a tap starts or stops the auto-turn. Hold it for the speed and style.
- **Phones stay cool:** the frame governor stays awake only while she moves, twirls, turns or the skirt is still swinging. 15 s after that it drops to 4 fps.
- **The designer's tape re-fits the mannequin** (`gownRefitTo`, for "Open on the mannequin"):
  - Waist to floor (or nape to waist) re-fits her legs and torso.
  - Shoulder to bust point re-fits the bust position.
  - Each other tape number is kept for the pattern.
  - The mannequin's own nape to waist and front length now run a tape down her body.
- **Checked against prototype v206:**
  - Every pose on her: every joint turn, the arm lifts, the hand-on-hip reach, where she stands, her body and the skirt on it.
  - A blend halfway.
  - Each walk and the jump at seven moments.
  - The v175 re-fit example, word for word.
