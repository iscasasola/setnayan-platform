## 2026-11-13 · fix(money): a Suppliers-only delegate reads no booking amount (#5936) — the readers (migration A of two)

Starts closing the route Build 3 lane B round 4 (migration `20271333705710`) left open for the owner: the five price columns on `event_vendors` — `total_cost_php` · `deposit_paid_php` · `transport_php` · `food_allowance_php` · `pax_surcharge_php` — ride on the same row a Suppliers delegate must read, so a delegate with Suppliers but no Budget & payments could read every booking's price.

The service role reads every amount. Purely additive: it closes no column.

Migration B publishes only after production is confirmed serving this commit.
