### Gown maker: shoulder straps, a scalloped top edge, a plunge to the waist, a veil that stays outside the skirt (prototype v199)

- Straps: spaghetti, wide, off-shoulder, one-shoulder, halter (meeting at the nape with a covered button) or cross-back, in the gown colour or the accent colour, plain or beaded. Each is laid on her own skin for her pose: pulled taut, 3 mm off the skin, its ends tucked 1.2 cm under the bodice. A V held by straps gets a camisole line.
- A plunge to the waist (`plungewaist`): sweetheart cups with a deep V between them, always on straps, filled with sheer modesty mesh or left open (`plungeFill`).
- A scalloped top edge (`topEdge`) on a strapless sweetheart or straight-across neckline, cut out of the gown's top.
- Straps that hold the bodice (spaghetti, wide, halter, cross-back) lift the strapless bra-line back cap; a strap on a shoulder replaces a cape's narrow strap there. The rules follow v199 word for word (halter and one-shoulder take no cap, flutter or butterfly sleeves; a high neck, a bateau or an illusion neckline takes no straps; off-shoulder straps only with off-shoulder or sweetheart), and the server refuses what they rule out. New quantities: the straps (`straps`) and the scallops (`scallop`).
- The veil keeps 4 mm outside the skirt as drawn (and its overlay and train) all the way to the floor; the old 1 cm dip into the A-line is gone. The gown's top edge is tidied over the shoulders, and strapless cups stand up above the bust point.
- Every case is held to v199 (the strap lines and every strap vertex within 1 mm, the necklines, the zone map and the scallops, the veil within 1 mm, the rules word for word). Beaded straps put their beads after the strap's own vertices (v199 interleaves them, which tangles the strap's faces).
- Dev lab only (`/dev/gown-maker-lab?view=dress`: Straps, Strap colour, Strap edge, Top edge and Plunge filled with pickers); nothing a person sees yet.
