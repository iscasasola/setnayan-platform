## 2026-09-28 · fix(logo): a logo is centred by its ink · every stage font on the Logo · position sliders snap to centre

**Centred by its ink.** Owner, of an "AM" logo sitting left of centre above the names on the hero: *"when a logo is created and it is not centered. you should automatically center it and not rely on how they aligned it to the left"*. A Logo-editor composition's viewBox was the editor's whole 1000² artboard, so every surface centred the ARTBOARD. `centreLogoOnItsInk` (`lib/logo-layers.ts`) re-frames the file on the union of every layer's ink — outlines measured at each curve's own extrema (`pathDataBounds`, `lib/print-mark.ts`; an italic swash reaches past its advance box and past its handles' hull), a kept white card / a frame by the box it paints — plus an even 4% margin. Derived on read inside `resolveEventMonogramSvg`, so every saved logo and every surface (hero 1–4, Reveal/seal, invite crest, QR centre, poster, app icon, social card, vendor brief) gets it with no migration; the layers themselves are byte-identical, so the arrangement and Draw on are untouched. Print already centred by bounds; those bounds are now the exact ink box too.

**Every stage font on the Logo.** Owner: *"on the logo. we need to show all fonts as well like in stages"*. The text layer's Typeface is now the stages' own PickMenu over `HUB_ELEMENT_FONTS` (35 faces, each name in its face) — one list. `lib/logo-fonts.ts` maps each to a TrueType outline file; 28 were decoded from the very WOFF2 the stages load (`scripts/make-logo-outline-fonts.py`, variable faces pinned to the stage weight, Latin-cut; `public/logo-fonts/`). Studio-era layers keep their face (`cardo` without `italic` = Cardo Italic; `cinzeldec` → `cinzeldeco`). Found on the way: Great Vibes and Gilda threw on ANY two letters (opentype.js always runs `ccmp` and cannot read its chained lookup) — `outlineWords` sets the words glyph by glyph when the face's layout throws.

**Snap to centre.** Owner, of Across / Up and down: *"allow snap to center here"*. A drag within 3% of the middle lands on exactly 500, with a centre tick on the track and a light haptic; the arrow keys step freely.

Tests: `lib/a-logo-is-centred-by-its-ink.test.ts` (rendered pixels: off-centre swash "AM" centred ±1px, not clipped; handle-overshoot curve centred ±1px; layers byte-identical; resolver/gate/app-icon), `lib/logo-fonts.test.ts`.
