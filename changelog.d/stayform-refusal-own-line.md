## 2026-11-10 · fix(stay-form): a refused save gets its own full-width line in every flex row, app-wide (Build 5 F1)

`StayForm` / `StayConfirmForm` print a refusal (`<p role="alert" data-stay-error>`) as a SIBLING of their form, so in any flex ROW it became one more flex item and crushed its neighbours — the Shop card row collapsed to a one-word column (owner screenshot 10 Nov, "Toggle issue"; that one row was patched in #7427). Fixed once at the source in `app/globals.css`: a `.flex` / `.inline-flex` row (not `.flex-col`) that is holding a refusal wraps (`:has(> [data-stay-error])`), and the refusal takes `flex-basis: 100%` on the last line; in a grid it spans every column. Rows without a refusal, and column parents, do not change. Survey: 209 StayForm uses, 73 sit directly in a flex row. Measured in Chromium + WebKit at 375 px on a lab of the real rule: the #7427 card-row shape without its own patch went from card width 0 → 274 px with the refusal 341/341 px underneath; a nowrap button row and a grid also get a full line; a column is unchanged. #7427's per-row classes stay (redundant, still green). Guard `app/_components/stay-form-refusal-own-line.test.ts` (5 tests; fails without the rule and with `order` broken).
