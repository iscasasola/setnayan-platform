## 2026-11-05 · feat(admin): a shop's service cards on its admin page — take a card down (and put it back), remove a cover or showcase photo

The admin minimum for the Saturday 7 Nov supplier registration: the audit found every piece present except a way to SEE a shop's service cards and to take one down (only whole-shop demote / archive / reject existed).

- **Admin › a supplier (`/admin/vendors/<id>#cards`) — "Service cards".** Every card of the shop, live, draft or taken down: cover, title, kind, price, a status chip, the showcase photos as thumbnails, and "Open the public card" (only when couples can actually open it). A refused read says "Couldn't read…", never "No service cards yet".
- **Take down card** — with a reason (10–500 characters, the supplier reads it word for word). Sets the card's existing public switch `is_active = false` — the switch the public read policy, the shop page `/v/[slug]` and Find already read — plus a hold the database will not let the supplier lift or publish past (new `vendor_services.taken_down_at` + `taken_down_reason`, trigger `a_taken_down_card_stays_down`). The supplier can still edit and save it as a draft; trying to publish it returns Setnayan's reason.
- **Put back** — lifts the hold and returns the card to what it was: live if it was live before (read from the take-down's audit row) and it still passes the publish gate; otherwise a draft, and the admin is told which.
- **Remove photo** — the cover or one showcase photo, with a reason. The photo is detached from the card; the file stays in storage and its key is in the audit row, so it can be restored by hand.
- **Every action** is admin-only (`requireAdminAction`, before any read), audited in `admin_audit_log` (`vendor_service_taken_down` · `vendor_service_put_back` · `vendor_service_photo_removed`, with actor and reason; counted on the Overview's Shops tile), and tells the supplier in-app — new notice type `service_card_moderated`, deliberately NOT on the email allowlist (no new email).
- **Reachable** from Accounts › Vendors ("Service cards" on every shop) and from Suppliers to verify (both the application card and the visibility desk: "See their service cards").
- **Lab** `/dev/card-moderation-lab` (dev-only, 404 in production): the real admin section and the real public services gallery on fixture data — `?f=admin`, `?f=admin&result=taken-down`, `?f=public`, `?f=public&after=1`. Added to the root map's no-door baseline (a lab has no way in from the app, like every other lab).
- Server-action budget 1,227 → 1,228: the three operations are ONE dispatcher (`moderateServiceCard`).
- Guards: `tests/db/a-taken-down-card-stays-down.db.test.ts` (the hold, against a replayed schema — a couple can read the card before and cannot after; the shop's switch, PATCH and the maker's RPC cannot bring it back; Setnayan can) · `lib/admin-can-take-a-card-down.test.ts` (admin gate first, audit + notice per op, in-app only, public readers read `is_active`, reachable).
