## 2026-10-20 · fix(invitation): the invitation prints a guest's full name — "Shown as" never replaces it

Owner, verbatim, on the live cale-ice entourage reading "Frocie · MOTHER OF THE GROOM": *"why the nickname replaced on the invitation. do not change the invitation. used the full name."*

**Cause.** `guestFullName` (`lib/guests.ts`), the one formal-name resolver behind the entourage (every look), the Wedding March, Me / Dress with us ("You walk first, with …"), /everyone, the printed Entourage card and the printed cards, returned the couple's `display_name` first whenever it was set. That was deliberate from the day the entourage shipped (#5487 / #5492, 2026-09-14: "The couple's own display_name still WINS"). The guest card's "Shown as (optional)" box (#6192, 2026-09-30, placeholder "e.g. Tito Boy & Tita Cora") asks for exactly a nickname, so a label typed for the couple's own list ended up printed on the invitation.

**Fix.** `guestFullName` now prints the five parts (prefix · first · middle · last · suffix, in the event's Name style) whenever there are any. It reads `display_name` only for a row that has no name parts at all. A new `guestNameAsGiven` keeps "Shown as" where the ruling keeps it: the couple's Guest list rows, and words addressed to the guest (the invite message `{name}`, the greeting on the invitation they open). The find-seat door pass now prints `ticketName` in the event's Name style, the same as the Digital and Printed ticket. Tickets and place cards were already formal and are unchanged.

**Guard.** `lib/the-invitation-prints-the-full-name.test.ts` uses a fixture in the owner's exact row shape (Atty. Eufrocina M. Sacdalan-Casasola, Shown as "Frocie"). It checks the full name, and never a nickname, on: the resolver in all three Name styles, the entourage in every look (default · Roll call · Lines · March, with the reader marked), pair lines, the name list, the "You walk first, with …" line, the printed Entourage card, the printed card, the ticket and the place card. It also scans the source: `guestNameAsGiven` may only be called from the reviewed doors, and no guest-facing file may read a guest's `.display_name` outside a reviewed list. Sabotaged three ways (resolver display-first → 5 red; a new `display_name` read in the entourage section → red; a new `guestNameAsGiven` caller → red), and restored green.
