### Gown maker: sheer illusion bodice, illusion back, boning you can see (prototype v198)

- "Bodice made of": the gown fabric, an illusion neckline (lined cups, sheer tulle above them up to the neckline) or a sheer corset (only the cups lined, sheer down to the waistline). Lined cups sweetheart, straight across or plunge; tulle nude, ivory or the gown colour. Lace sits on top of the tulle; a bound edge where the lining ends.
- An illusion back: sheer tulle closes an open back up to the top edge. On a strapless bodice it lifts the bra-line limit; turning it off raises the back to the bra line again.
- "Boning you can see": thin channels from the waistline up, clear through tulle and faint on the gown fabric. One bone list (7 to 13 bones by body and boning) now feeds Inside, Boning, the boning metres and the channels.
- The rules follow v198 (an illusion neckline on a strapless neckline becomes bateau; a sheer corset or visible boning adds boning; a soft bodice hides it; a closed back has no illusion back; a strapless low back gets one), and the server refuses what they rule out. The illusion tulle's metres and the bodice's work are new quantities.
- Every case is held to v198 (the sheer zone's lines, the bones and their channels, the tulle metres and the designer's lines exact).
- Dev lab only (`/dev/gown-maker-lab?view=dress`, the Bodice made of, Lined cups, Tulle colour, Illusion back, Boning and Boning you can see pickers); nothing a person sees yet.
