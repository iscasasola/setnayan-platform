## 2026-11-12 · fix(money): a Suppliers-only delegate reads no booking amount (#5936)

Closes the route Build 3 lane B round 4 (migration `20271333705710`) left open for the owner: the five price columns on `event_vendors` — `total_cost_php` · `deposit_paid_php` · `transport_php` · `food_allowance_php` · `pax_surcharge_php` — rode on the same row a Suppliers delegate must read, so a delegate with Suppliers but no Budget & payments could read every booking's price straight from PostgREST.

- **Migration `20271341392450` (not applied anywhere — the pipeline pushes it):** the five columns are closed per column to `anon` and `authenticated` (every other column keeps exactly the SELECT it had; UPDATE/INSERT untouched). `event_vendor_money_event_ids()` names the events whose amounts the caller may read — a host, or a delegate whose Budget & payments area is not Off (`current_moderator_area_levels('budget')`, the same grant `money_hidden_event_ids()` checks), never a coordinator that predicate hides. `event_vendor_money_read(event_id)` is the ONE reader: the rows the table's own two SELECT doors admit, amounts filled only on those events, `money_shown = false` meaning hidden — never ₱0. The service role reads every amount.
- **App:** `lib/event-vendor-money.ts` (`readBookingMoney` · `readOneBookingMoney` · `withBookingMoney`). Every user-session read that named an amount now selects without it and merges from the reader: `lib/vendors.ts` (`fetchEventVendors`), `lib/budget.ts` (both), `lib/budget-truth.ts` (`resolveEventMoney`, whose `select('*')` fallback now names its columns), `lib/checklist-budget.ts`, `lib/pax.ts`, `lib/setnayan-ai-activity.ts`, the event dashboard, the supplier workspace page, `vendors/actions.ts` (four reads), `payment-plan-actions.ts`, `budget/actions.ts`, `build-3state-actions.ts`.
