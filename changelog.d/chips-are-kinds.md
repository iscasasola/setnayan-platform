## 2026-08-20 · change(frontdoor): the chip row becomes the KINDS on the shelf — and the search row stops underselling itself

Owner 2026-08-20, self-contradictory (ARTICLES appeared in both his keep list
and his remove list): *"we want this to be ALL, YOUR PEOPLE, ARTICLES,
MARKETPLACE / remove ARTICLES, THEIR STORIES, WITH VIDEO / does this work
better? since the search bar on top will handle those? / Stories, Articles and
Marketplace"*. Then: *"use Fable to help us plan this. also find across the
internet."*

**Result: `All · Your people · Stories · Articles`.**

### What his reasoning got right, and where it was measured wrong

*"The search bar will handle those"* was **true of the destination and false of
the row offering it.** `/explore?q=` has answered suppliers **and** stories
**and** guides since 2026-08-15 — verified live: `?q=doves` returns the doves
guide under a "Stories and guides" heading. But the signed-in palette's only
escape row said **"Find suppliers for X"**, so nobody would press it for a
guide. **So this is a LABEL fix, not a feature** — the row now reads *"Search
Setnayan for X · Suppliers, stories and guides"*, derived from the signed-out
box's own noun list so the two can never promise different things again.

### What came off

- ⛔ **"With video"** — a MODIFIER on a story, not a KIND on the shelf; the one
  chip not parallel with its neighbours. Nothing is lost: every card still
  carries its "▶ with video" badge, so a person can still SEE which have video.
- 🏷 **"Their stories" → "Stories"** — the possessive only earned its place as a
  contrast to "Your people" beside it. The owner wrote the row back to us using
  "Stories", and it matches the hub's address and the menu row it replaced.

### What was refused, with the reasoning pinned in a test

⛔ **Marketplace is not a chip.** It is a different ROOM, not a kind of reading,
and it already has **three doors** (the shops rail, the rail destination, the
search row). It would also be the only chip that NAVIGATES rather than FILTERS.
Precedent: Instagram removed the Shop tab from its content home in 2023;
TikTok's push of shop content into the feed drew measurable backlash; NN/g on
duplicate links — indiscriminate extra doors deplete attention.
📉 And the local arithmetic agrees: **two shops exist; the one with services is
hidden, and the one a stranger can reach has ZERO services.**

### ⚖ Where the planning pass was overruled

Fable recommended dropping **"Your people"** too, on the sound general ground
that a filter returning nothing reads as broken. **Overruled: the owner ruled on
that chip hours earlier, after being told in plain terms that it would be empty
for every account** — and kept it in all three variants of this very message.
A reaffirmed decision is the owner's to make. It also already renders a written
invitation rather than a blank shelf, which is what the empty-state evidence
actually asks for.
