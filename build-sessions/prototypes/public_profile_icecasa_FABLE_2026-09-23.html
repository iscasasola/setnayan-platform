Note for the owner — not part of the page

What I changed, and why

  • The page sits on warm paper, not white, and the name and the celebration names are set large in a serif. That is most of why it stopped looking pale. The page still has no photos; the colour now comes from the celebrations themselves.
  • Each celebration is a card with a cover, like invitations laid on a table. The cover is coloured with the celebration's own accent: wine for Indalecio & Claire, gold for Maria & Jose. Their themes decorate the cover: the wine card carries a capiz-window lattice and two leaf sprigs (botanical); the gold card uses the default theme, so it gets only a formal double frame.
  • Every card has something in the middle. Where a couple chose a monogram, the cover shows it (I & C). Where they did not, the cover shows the date on a paper disc instead, so no card looks less finished than another.
  • Movie Night has no colour and no theme, so it wears the house's own paper: a hairline frame, a faint linen grain, and its date on a white disc. It is meant to read as plain, good card stock, not as something missing. It is the same card as the gold one with the colour left off.
  • The Setnayan bar is back, at its smallest: the mark, "Made with Setnayan", and Sign in. Nothing about suppliers. It uses the page's own colour so it feels like part of the person's page rather than a toolbar sitting on top of it.
  • The gold accent (#9b7e00) cannot carry text and pass 4.5:1 (white on it is 3.9, near-black on it is 3.7), so on the gold card the words never sit on the gold. They sit on the white disc and in the caption below. Every text colour on this page was checked the same way; the worst pair is the grey on paper at 4.8.

What I am not sure about

  • "0 followers · 1 view" is real, but on a page someone sends to their family it reads a little sad. I kept it small. You may prefer to hide counts while they are zero.
  • "Cale-Ice" is the couple's moodboard name. I set it under their monogram as a small signature because it is theirs. If moodboard names are meant to stay private, drop that one line.
  • Movie Night's date has passed. I left the order as it is and added no "past" label; whether finished celebrations sort last, or get a quiet label, is your call and I did not want to invent it.
  • The Setnayan mark in the bar is a placeholder shape. I did not have the real logo file.
  • On option B: I think it is the right shell, with two small conditions. It should not stick to the top while you scroll, and it should not be white on a paper page. A stranger's first impression should be the person's name, and the bar should be the second thing they notice.
