## 2026-07-27 · feat(vendor): the free-travel ring becomes ENFORCED — a quote can no longer bill transport inside the range the vendor declared free

Completes **#3816**, which shipped the two rings, the vendor's settings fields and the couple's three-state badge — and left the money half unbuilt. After #3816 a vendor could tell couples *"no travel fee within 15 km of our HQ"*, have that render as **"No travel fee"** on the couple's bench, and then send the very same couple a quote with ₱15,000 of *"Transportation"* on it. Nothing checked. The badge was a promise with no rule behind it.

Salvaged from the abandoned, never-PR'd `origin/claude/vendor-two-ring-reach` (`a8d5f25a8`), which had independently built the same two-ring model and — alone — the server-side enforcement. Only the enforcement is taken; that branch's own migration, columns, flag and vendor UI are superseded by #3816 and are **not** brought across.

**Why the server, and only the server.** The Proposal Maker composes its line items **in the browser** and POSTs them as JSON. Hiding, disabling or zeroing the transportation field client-side is a UX affordance, not an enforcement boundary — a crafted request arrives at `sendCustomProposalCore` carrying whatever its author wants. So the rewrite sits at that one chokepoint, which every vendor-authored quote goes through, web action and native endpoint alike.

**The rewrite re-totals, and that is not a detail.** `applyFreeTransportToQuote` (`lib/vendor-free-transport.ts`) zeroes the transportation line **and re-sums the quote from the enforced lines, as one indivisible function.** Rewriting a line without re-summing would persist `total_centavos` against an itemization that adds up to less — the couple accepts a quote whose lines and headline disagree, and the headline is the number the booking-fee and payment-schedule maths read, so they pay the travel fee anyway, just without a line item naming it. Duplicate transportation lines collapse to one; an omitted one is **added** at ₱0 so the promise is visible rather than inferred from an absence.

**THE INVARIANT — the badge the couple sees and the rule the vendor is held to read the same number.** Both now go through **one** composer, `resolveDeclaredRings` in `lib/vendor-service-radius.ts`, and both read the **CLAMPED** radii (`effectiveInnerRadiusKm` / `effectiveOuterRadiusKm`), never the raw declared columns. That is what makes it coherent across a subscription change: a vendor who declared 30 km of free travel on Pro and lapses to Verified (20 km cap) shows couples a 20 km free ring — enforcing on the declared 30 would force free travel they no longer advertise to anyone, the platform confiscating a fee on the strength of a promise it had stopped showing. `app/dashboard/[eventId]/vendors/page.tsx` was moved onto the shared composer (behaviour-identical; it previously composed the two helpers by hand) so the promise and the rule cannot drift apart by a copy-paste.

**Flag-dark and fail-soft.** New dedicated flag `NEXT_PUBLIC_VENDOR_FREE_TRANSPORT_ENFORCED`, default **OFF** — deliberately *not* `NEXT_PUBLIC_EXPLORE_REPLAN_ENABLED`, because this one changes what a couple is charged and a layout rollout must not silently start rewriting money. Off, `resolveThreadFreeTransport` returns null **before issuing a single query** and `applyFreeTransportToQuote(lines, null)` returns the same lines and the same total `sanitizeCustomLineItems` already produced — byte-identical behaviour, zero extra round-trips on the send path. It also returns null on **any** error: a transient PostgREST blip must never silently confiscate a legitimate travel fee, nor fail the send. **Undeclared rings mean no enforcement** — we never invent a free ring for a vendor who drew none.

**No UI, on purpose.** The verdict never reaches the vendor's browser — not the pin, not the distance, not the ring. The vendor controls *both* inputs to the comparison (their own inner-radius field and their own HQ pin), so an on-demand "is this venue inside?" readout is a **trilateration oracle**: a handful of saves pins the distance from one origin and two HQ moves locate the couple's venue to ~1 km, against something the couple never disclosed. `resolveThreadFreeTransport` therefore has exactly one caller and its result is consumed and discarded. Making it safe to *show* needs a different design — snapshot at accept time, from inputs the vendor can no longer move.

The vendor read is column-explicit (`tier_state,inner_radius_km,outer_radius_km,hq_latitude,hq_longitude`) and isolated in its own query, never folded into an existing select — a not-yet-migrated column answers 42703 and nulls the whole row.

**One latent divergence fixed on the way past.** `travelFeeVerdictForVendor` (currently unused in production — only its own tests call it) composed the rings *differently* from the page that actually renders the badge: it passed `effectiveOuterRadiusKm(null, tier)`, i.e. the **tier cap**, for a vendor who never declared an outer ring, so an inner-declared-only vendor would have read `free_travel` there while the bench correctly read "not declared". It now delegates to `resolveDeclaredRings` like everything else. No shipped surface changes; a trap sitting one import away from the new enforcement does not.

Tests — new `lib/vendor-free-transport.test.ts` (22 cases). The headline pair drive the **real badge function** (`resolveReachBadge`, composed exactly as the bench composes it) and the **real enforcement** over a 17-case grid — inclusive boundaries, `inner = 0`, `inner === outer`, each ring undeclared alone, unknown distance, and every tier — asserting the biconditional in both directions: badge "No travel fee" ⟺ the transport line is zeroed, badge "Travel fee applies" ⟺ the vendor keeps their line. Plus the clamp across a lapse, the re-total, duplicate collapse, the flag-dark no-op, freebie (null-amount) lines, and source-level wiring pins. Every assertion was **falsified** before being kept: enforcing on the raw declared columns → 3 red; dropping the re-sum → 3 red; deleting the call site → 1 red; re-inlining the bench composer → 1 red; resurrecting the booking-fee gate → 1 red. (The first pass of the wiring pin was itself found broken during falsification — it matched the *comment* naming the function; assertions now read comment-stripped source.)

⚠ **`bookingFeeSendGate` stays retired.** It was deliberately retired 2026-07-24 (the fee now fires once, on the LOCK path via `collectBookingFeeAtLock`) and has no non-test caller, which makes it look like an oversight to anyone editing the send path. This PR does not re-enable it, and pins that with a test rather than a comment — a comment is one delete away from gone.

No migration, no schema change, no new columns: this PR is pure enforcement over what #3816 already landed.

⚠ Recommend deleting `origin/claude/vendor-two-ring-reach` once this merges — it conflicts with main and everything still live in it is now either superseded by #3816 or salvaged here.
