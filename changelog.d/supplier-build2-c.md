## 2026-11-08 · feat(billing): Build 2 · B2-C — a supplier's Billing & receipts holds every supplier list; readable receipt inks; the chat ring unclipped; the ledger door from Payment; the export's staff uid becomes a label

Build 2 lane B2-C (corpus `plans/supplier_build_2_2026-11-08/`, prototype v118). The goal is "make it better" — every Build 1 behaviour keeps working.

- **#39 · Billing & receipts holds every supplier list** (owner 8 Nov: *"Add them to purchases and receipts"*). The shipped `/dashboard/billing` keeps its Purchases list for everyone, unchanged. A login standing in a shop also gets **Gifts given · Payments received · Refunds · Rewards earned** for that shop, under ONE dropdown (All + the five sections; picking hides the others, no refetch, `?show=` deep links). Section headings stick under the pinned row: the title, one short line, and the details in the ⓘ. Every list draws the shipped row (`BillingRow`, extracted from `billing-list.tsx`, same markup). A gift opens that client on **Quotation**; a payment opens that client on **Payment** (where "Their receipt" and Confirm / Not received live); a refund opens the slip it reverses; a reward opens the shop's badges. A refused read says "didn't load", never the empty line.
  - **Reads** (`lib/supplier-billing.server.ts`): seven requests in ONE `Promise.all`, run beside the shipped purchases read, so the page takes two round trips end to end. Gift lines, given stock, badge rewards and payments are read on the supplier's own **session** client, each filtered to the active shop. The shop's bookings and the client names are read on the admin client, scoped by the shop the session resolved. That is the sanctioned shape: a supplier has no RLS on `event_vendors` or `events`. Both `events` embeds name their foreign key (`events!event_vendors_event_id_fkey`, `events!vendor_proposals_event_id_fkey`), because a bare `events(` from `event_vendors` is PGRST201 in production.
  - **RULE 0:** badge rewards already live in `shop_badge_rewards` (migration `20271323363853`). RLS already admits a shop's own gift lines, stock, rewards and payments. **No migration, no new table.** Proven by `tests/db/a-supplier-reads-only-its-own-billing.db.test.ts`: a shop reads its own rows and none of another shop's, and a login in two shops reads both, which is why the reader filters by the shop.
  - Refunds come from the shipped purchases read (the shop's own refunded orders), never a second read.
  - Lab: `/dev/billing-lab?supplier=1` (`&show=gifts|payments|refunds|rewards` · `&supplier=unread`).
- **#41 · The receipt stamp ink is readable.** PAID was 2.47:1 on the slip paper and is now 5.09:1 (a darker green; opacity .9 instead of .75; the same worn mask and tilt). Guard: `receipt-ink-is-readable.test.ts`, which computes both ratios from the exported constants.
